1. WHY THE WIP PAGE WAS EMPTY WHILE PROJECT FINANCIALS SHOWED A WIP FIGURE
===========================================================================
They read different keys. pages/api/wip.js pulls

    costs:lines:<xeroId>
    invoiced:lines:<xeroId>
    wip:adjustments:<xeroId>

directly. The dashboard cache's _costLines and _invoiceLines feed the
Commercial table and the WIP page never looks at them. The same data, needed
in two places under two names - which is why one screen had it and the other
did not.

All three keys are now written per project, and added to the wipe list.

16 of 30 projects have post-valuation costs in the August window, about 97k of
cost, which grosses up to roughly 129k of WIP.


2. THE MARGIN LEAK
===================
19.1% against a target of 25%. The cause was my own post-valuation spike: I
was adding uninvoiced cost in the 29th-31st window of EVERY month. For any
month before August those dates fall before the current valuation date, so
they counted as costs-to-date with no matching revenue.

Now only the last two periods carry it.

Measured: 23.4%. The remaining gap is CORRECT rather than a compromise - WIP
is by definition cost incurred and not yet invoiced, so
(invoiced - costs) / invoiced always reads a little under the true margin
while work is in progress. The underlying margin is 25%; the reported figure
sits just below it because there is genuine WIP on the books, which is what a
real contractor's number does.


3. CREDIT NOTES
================
About one project in five now has one - a contra charge, a measurement
correction, a rejected item. They are invoice lines with a negative value and
creditNote: true, carrying appliedToInvoice and a contact, which is the shape
pages/api/wip.js looks for when it lists credit notes against a project.
Six across 30 projects in the current dataset.


YOUR OTHER QUESTIONS
====================

CERTIFIED NOT EDITABLE INLINE. That is the app working correctly, not a
seeding gap. The cell shows "app true" - meaning the figure came from a SENT
application, and the code deliberately stops a typed value overriding one.
The comment in pages/api/dashboard.js explains it: "A typed value is now only
used where no sent application exists", added because an override entered once
sat on top of every later application. On Rock you are editing projects that
have no sent application against them. If you want it editable in the demo,
the answer is to seed some projects with no applications - say the word.

ONE PROJECT WITH INVOICED 0.00 (J104). A genuinely new job whose first
application falls after the valuation date. Its costs are zero too, so it does
NOT drag the margin - it contributes nothing to either side. Realistic, and I
would leave it, but it can be removed by forcing every project to be at least
two months old.

RECEIPTS. Not addressed here - "receipts" in this app means uploaded supplier
documents against cost lines, which is Blob storage plus submission records.
That belongs with the forms-and-photos package.
