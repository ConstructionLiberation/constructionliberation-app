PACKAGE 1077 - Tech Subs: several customer approvers, and proof they were told

Two files. Complete replacements. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1076.

  pages/api/design-techsubs.js
  pages/design/[project]/tech-sub.js

YOUR QUESTION: does issuing a Tech Sub notify the customer?
YES - adding a Tech Sub, or a new revision, has always emailed the chosen
customer automatically (there is no separate button, unlike drawings). But
before this package there was NO RECORD of it, and a failed send - or a
customer with no email address on their user - was silently swallowed, so it
looked exactly like a customer who had been told. That is fixed below.

1. SEVERAL CUSTOMERS
The approver window is now a tick list - tick as many customer contacts as
needed. Each is emailed; ANY ONE of them can approve. A new revision starts
with the previous revision's approvers ticked. A customer with no email
address is marked as such in the list.

2. HOW YOU KNOW THEY WERE NOTIFIED
  - Straight after adding: a box at the top says who was emailed and anyone
    who was not, with why ("No email address on their user", a send failure).
  - On every Tech Sub, under Approval: the approvers' names, and
    "Notified 2 · 11 Oct 14:05" - hover for each person's result - or
    "1 not sent" in amber.
  - Every send is recorded on the Tech Sub (who, when, each result).

3. NOTIFY AGAIN
On any current Tech Sub not yet approved: "Notify again" - tick the customers,
Send. Re-sends, records the result, and those ticked become its approvers.

Also fixed: adding a Tech Sub or revision now checks the server's reply - it
was ignored, so a failed save looked like success.

Older Tech Subs with a single approver keep working.

Checked through the real route: a Tech Sub added for two customers - one
emailed, the other reported "No email address on their user", recorded, and
only the real address sent to; a non-approver refused, the SECOND approver able
to approve; Notify again to two customers - both emailed, approvers updated, a
second record; notify with nobody refused; an older single-approver Tech Sub
approved by its approver. Both files bundle; all 158 pages pass the browser
check.

SMOKE TEST
1. Design -> a project -> Tech Sub -> Add -> tick two customers -> choose a
   file. The box at the top lists who was emailed.
2. The row shows the approvers and "Notified 2 · <time>".
3. Notify again -> Send - a new time.
