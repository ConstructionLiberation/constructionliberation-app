PACKAGE 1072 - Application PDF: summary headings no longer overlap

One file. Complete replacement. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1071.

  lib/applicationPdf.js

THE FAULT
The Summary table's column headings were drawn on one line, right-aligned to
columns 108pt wide. "Application for Payment Total" needs about 125pt, so it
ran left into "Contract Sum" ("Contract SumApplication for Payment Total").

THE FIX
A heading too wide for its column wraps onto a second line, right-aligned,
and the grey heading band grows to fit:

              Contract Sum   Application for Payment    Proj. Final Account
                                               Total

It fits whatever the heading says - the claim name follows the company's
terms (e.g. "Payment Claim Total" in New Zealand), so no fixed width would do.

Used by both Download PDF and the PDF attached when an application is sent.

Checked: a real application PDF generated and read back - every heading word
in its own space, "Contract Sum" ending at 327pt and "Application for Payment"
starting at 342pt, "Total" on the second line, nothing overlapping or run
together, divider lines drawn. (The test also caught and fixed a clash in my
first version: a name I used was already the divider-line colour.) Both PDF
routes bundle; all 158 pages pass the browser check.
