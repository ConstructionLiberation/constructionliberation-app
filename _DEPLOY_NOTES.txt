WHY THIS, AND NOT BUSINESS FINANCIALS
================================================================================
955 fixed the logo in the four NAV components, which covers most of the
portal. It did not fix the pages that build their own header - and two of
those are pages WPC will definitely open. Rock Roofing's actual logo has been
sitting at the top of demo's CRM and demo's Bookkeeping this whole time,
above figures we have just spent the day converting to dollars.

That is worth more than an empty Business Financials, and it is the same
finding as 19 September: the leak is never where the sweep is pointed.

Six of these eight I had OPEN in 960, 962 and 963 and changed the currency in
without touching the title tag. Worth saying plainly - being in the file is
not the same as looking at it.
