PACKAGE 1066 - WIP complete email: Accounts users only

One file. Complete replacement. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1065.

  pages/api/wip-lock.js

When the WIP is marked complete, the email now goes to ACTIVE users whose
access role is ACCOUNTS - nobody else.

  Before   everyone with Bookkeeping access: Accounts, Management and Admin,
           including DEACTIVATED users who still had an email on record
  Now      Accounts users only, and only if active

To add or remove someone: change their access role in Admin > Users. The
pop-up after marking complete still lists exactly who was sent it.

If nobody has the Accounts role, the WIP still locks but no email is sent -
the pop-up says nobody was notified.

Checked: the route run with a mix of users - an active Accounts user emailed;
a deactivated Accounts user, a director, an admin, a deactivated leaver, a
post-contract user and an Accounts user with no email address all left out;
the pop-up lists the same one recipient. The route bundles.
