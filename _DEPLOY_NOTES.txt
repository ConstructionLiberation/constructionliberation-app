PACKAGE 980 - so tomorrow's error is worth reading
================================================================================

One file. Complete replacement.

  lib/xero.js

Deploy with 979. No overlap.


WHY
================================================================================
Xero being connected rules out the obvious cause, so 977's message tomorrow
is now the whole diagnosis - and half of it would still have been useless:

    if (!res.ok) throw new Error('Token refresh failed')

No status, no body. Every caller that reports that message tells you
nothing. It now throws:

    Token refresh failed: 400 {"error":"invalid_grant"}

Which matters because the three likely bodies are three different jobs:

  invalid_grant   the refresh token has been consumed or revoked. Xero
                  ROTATES the refresh token on every use, so two concurrent
                  refreshes leave one caller holding a dead one.
  invalid_client  the credentials are wrong - relevant since the secret was
                  rotated on 19 September.
  429             rate limiting. Xero allows 60 calls a minute per tenant and
                  fetchPurchaseOrders pages at 100 POs a time.

fetchPurchaseOrders already reports properly - it throws the status and the
body - so with 977 in place tomorrow's alert will name whichever it is.


WHAT I CHECKED AND RULED OUT
================================================================================
A refresh-token RACE was the best hypothesis once you said Xero was still
connected: four crons fire at exactly 06:00, and Xero rotating the refresh
token means two simultaneous refreshes kill one of them.

It is not that. Of the four jobs at 06:00 - deliveries-sync, weekly-report,
hs-renewals, crm-daily-activities - only deliveries-sync touches Xero at
all, and it refreshes once.

Worth keeping in mind anyway: TWELVE call sites across the app refresh the
token, and most of them do it inside `catch {}`. Any two of those running
together - a cron while someone has the WIP page open - is the same race,
just not on a schedule. That is a real design issue for later, not tonight's
bug.
