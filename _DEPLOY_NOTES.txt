ONE THING TO LOOK AT
================================================================================
business-financials/export.js printed "Rock Roofing Limited" in two places -
the cover page of an exported financial pack and the footer of every section
after it. It now prints companyName, which for Rock is "Rock Roofing" - so
that document LOSES the word "Limited".

That is the legalName question, and this is the strongest argument yet for
splitting the field: a display name in a nav bar and a company name at the
top of a financial pack are not the same string. Same issue as
VariationBuilder's "Rock Roofing Limited" on a contractual document.

I have not invented a legalName field on my own. Say the word and it goes in
as one change across both places.

WHAT IS LEFT AFTER THIS
================================================================================
Two files, and they are a different shape:

  pages/operations/forms.js      _legacyPrintSubmissions()
  pages/operations/projects.js   openPrintView()

Both build a complete HTML document as a STRING for a print window, with
`${window.location.origin}/rock-logo.jpg` baked in and "Rock Roofing" in the
title. A React component is no use inside a string, so they need the logo URL
itself - which means handling both the relative and absolute forms, since
Rock's record currently holds an absolute one. And both are plain functions,
not components, so the value has to be threaded in from the caller.

That is 967, and it is the last of the client-side branding.
