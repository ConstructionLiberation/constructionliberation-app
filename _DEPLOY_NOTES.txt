PACKAGE 1021 - the approval block on the Site App report
================================================================================

One file. Complete replacement. Supersedes its copy in 1019 and 1020.
No re-seed.

  pages/forms/cm/reports.js


WHAT WAS MISSING
================================================================================
The Site App form had a bare "Your name" box. The portal has a proper
approval block: the heading, the declaration, the name and the date.

It now has the same one, WORD FOR WORD:

    APPROVAL
    I can confirm that the information I have provided is true and that I
    have completed all sections accurately and diligently.

    Name *   [                    ]
    Date     [ 2026-10-06         ]
    Defaults to today. Change it if the report is being written up for an
    earlier visit - this is the date it counts against on the Commercial
    Scorecard.

The wording is identical on purpose. It is a declaration: the person signing
it on a phone is signing the same words as the person signing it at a desk,
and two versions of that sentence drifting apart is not a cosmetic problem.


THE DATE IS THE PART THAT MATTERS
================================================================================
It was not on the Site App form at all, so every report written on site was
stamped with the day it was typed.

The portal's comment explains why that is wrong, and it applies more on a
phone than anywhere: a Friday walk round written up on Monday was dated
Monday, and the Commercial Scorecard counts reports by this date - so it
landed in the wrong week.

Behaviour checked: a date typed in is kept; left empty it defaults to today.
Same rule as the portal, which also only defaults when the field is genuinely
blank.

The read-only view now shows the approval date as well, since it is now
something somebody chose rather than a timestamp.


SMOKE TEST
================================================================================
1. Site App, new report: the approval block appears at the bottom with the
   declaration, name and date.
2. The date shows today by default.
3. Change it to last Friday and submit. Open the report in the PORTAL - the
   approval date is last Friday, not today.
4. View it in the Site App - the approval date shows.
5. Commercial Scorecard: the report counts against the week of the date you
   set, not the week you typed it.
