WHY THIS TOOK THREE GOES
================================================================================
I was fixing one input per package. The blanks were never separate problems.

lib/crmDashboardAdapter.js builds two date fields:

    wonTime:   f.won_time
    closeTime: f.won_time || f.lost_time

and the scorecard hangs FOUR metrics off them:

    monthWon         -> Total value of work secured        was $0
    rolling6         -> Strike rate, overall and MC        was blank
    avgSecuredWindow -> Average secured project value      was blank
    dealsOver200k    -> Deals secured over 200k            was 0

The seeder set status to 'won' or 'lost' and never said WHEN. Status alone is
not a decision - the date is. Every one of those filters matched nothing, so
four cards read as though the metric were broken rather than the data absent.

Now every won deal carries won_time and every lost deal lost_time, dated
between halfway through the deal's life and almost now. Not a fixed fraction:
that closes every old deal in the last month and leaves a trend line with one
spike. Checked the spread - decisions land across seven months, weighted to
recent, which is how a pipeline actually looks.


ONE THAT IS NOT A DEMO PROBLEM, AND WILL NOT BE FIXED BY SEEDING
================================================================================
"Value priced - existing customers" reads:

    deal?.customerType === 'Existing Customer'

and lib/crmDashboardAdapter.js line 108 is:

    customerType: null,

Hardcoded, with a note at the top of the file: "Per product decision:
customerType / firstContactDate / receivedDate are NOT in the Pipedrive export
and are left blank here - they will be captured going forward, not
back-filled."

So that metric returns 0 on EVERY tenant, Rock included, and always has. It
has a target of 300,000 and a red dot next to it on your own live scorecard.
No seeding can change that - it needs customerType captured on the deal, and
the field exists in the CRM schema.

Worth knowing the note is now partly stale: firstContactDate IS computed, at
line 104 of the same file. customerType is the one still hardcoded.


THE ROLE UNDER THE NAME
================================================================================
Each tab now shows the person's role in light grey beneath their name -
Estimator or Sales. Derived from which field they appear in on a deal, the
same source as the tab list itself, so it is correct on every tenant with
nothing to configure and no second place to keep in step.
