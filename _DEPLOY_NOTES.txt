THE SPLIT IS AT THIS MONTH
  Finished months     ACTUAL sales from Xero - the same figure as Total sales.
                      BLUE line, as on the other graphs.
  This month onward   the Forecast P&L's revenue for that month.
                      ORANGE line, starting on the last blue point so the two
                      join. Orange and italic in the table.

On 10 October that is actual Dec - Sep, forecast Oct - Nov. September counts
as actual even while Budgets still has it as a forecast month: it is
finished, and Xero has its figure.

A finished month with no Xero figures yet (not synced) uses its forecast
figure, is drawn orange, and is NAMED under the card - e.g. "Sep 26 not synced
from Xero yet - forecast used". It is never dropped from the total.


THE CARD
  The financial year's total sales: actual + forecast.
  Underneath, "2,200,119 actual + 521,852 forecast" in blue and orange, and
  the months each part covers.

This is the sum of the monthly sales. The Forecast P&L's WIP and year-end WIP
adjustments are not added - they belong to the margin, which the Forecast
gross margin card already includes.

Checked by running the route on the Forecast P&L screenshot's figures with
10 October as today: blue Dec-Sep, orange Oct-Nov (521,852), total
2,721,971 = hand sum. Then with September unsynced: forecast used and named.
