NEEDS "fyStartMonth": 12 ON tenant:rock (from 1032). If the page opens on
November rather than December, that line is missing - the page also says so
in a yellow notice.


THE FILTER
  From [month] to [month] at the top, replacing the period dropdown.
  Opens on the CURRENT FINANCIAL YEAR: December to November. The graphs and
  the table start in December.
  Change either month and every card follows. "This financial year" appears
  once you have moved off it, to jump back.
  Month dropdowns rather than a date box, because Firefox has no month picker.
  A range entered backwards is turned round, not refused.

THE BIG FIGURES - worked over the SELECTED PERIOD, finished months only
  Gross margin %  the margin for the period, from its totals - by default the
                  margin for the financial year to date, not the last month.
  Total sales     (renamed from Total invoiced) the total sales for the period
                  - by default the financial year to date.
  Labels: "FY2026 to date" / "FY2025 full year" when the range is a whole
  financial year, otherwise the months, e.g. "Jan 26 - Mar 26". The months
  actually counted are underneath.

  Months not finished (this month) or not yet reached (the rest of the year)
  are blank on the graph, not zero.

  The stored key for Total sales is unchanged, so any target already set is
  kept.

Checked by running the route against a made-up P&L: the default year, a past
year, Jan-Mar (37.5% on 400,000, checked by hand), a reversed range, and no
year start set.
