PACKAGE 938 - the documents that leave the building
================================================================================

Seven files. Complete replacements. Nothing new to create.

  lib/outstandingInvoicesPdf.js
  lib/approvalAudit.js
  lib/preStartNotifyPdf.js
  lib/signedRamsPdf.js
  lib/wipPdf.js
  lib/submissionsPdf.js

DEPENDS ON pkg937. designShell.js imports useFormat from
components/TenantProvider, which pkg937 creates. Deploy 937 first.


WHY THESE FILES FIRST
=====================
Of the 94 real "Rock Roofing" strings on the server, these are the ones printed
onto documents that a customer sends to THEIR client. A wrong company name in a
nav bar is embarrassing; a wrong company name on a digital approval certificate
or a RAMS signature record is a document that misrepresents who issued it.

They are also the hardest for you to notice, because nobody re-reads a PDF
footer.


WHAT CHANGED
============
Every one of these now calls companyName(), which reads the tenant record and
falls back to "Rock Roofing Ltd" when no tenant is in scope. So nothing changes
for Rock and everything changes for customer two.

  outstandingInvoicesPdf   page footer
  approvalAudit            certificate heading, body text, and footer -
                           THREE instances on a single legal-ish document
  preStartNotifyPdf        footer
  signedRamsPdf            page footer on the signature record
  wipPdf                   "Produced ... from the <name> portal"
  submissionsPdf           page footer
  designShell              the /rock-logo.jpg in the Design portal nav

designShell is a React component, so it uses useFormat() from the pkg937
provider rather than companyName(). The Design portal is the one area a
CUSTOMER'S OWN CLIENT logs in to, which makes it the last place another
company's logo should appear.


THE LOGO WILL VANISH FROM THE DESIGN NAV
=========================================
Same as the login page in pkg937: logoUrl is empty on Rock's tenant record, so
the img is omitted rather than rendering broken. Expect it. To restore:

  add "logoUrl": "https://<public url>" to tenant:rock

A blob-store URL is the right home, and it also fixes the cron-generated PDFs
that currently fetch the logo from the deployment URL and get blocked by
Deployment Protection.
