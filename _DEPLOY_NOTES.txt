COMMERCIAL > MATERIALS TAKE-OFFS - the job's take-off, re-measured for
construction without ever touching the quote.
  - pick the project (those whose contracted rates were built from an estimate
    show it); "START THE MATERIALS TAKE-OFF FROM Q-1042 REV A" copies that
    revision - the same one the contracted rates came from. A project not
    linked yet is pointed to Contracted Rates > Build from estimate.
  - TAKE-OFF tab: each item with its estimate quantity and the JOB QUANTITY
    (editable; purple once re-measured); LEAVE OUT / Put back an item the job
    does not need; BUILD-UP opens the same editor as Estimating - for this job
    only (the estimate and the library are not changed).
  - MATERIALS tab: the Estimating Materials tab itself, on the job's take-off
    (left-out items excluded) - suppliers, products, packs, order waste,
    allocations - so the packs come out exactly as in Estimating.
  - Save; the page shows how many items are re-measured or left out, and who
    saved last.
The job take-off is stored in the same shape as an estimate (its frozen prices
and its own material rates included); part 3 turns its Materials list into
draft POs, one per supplier.
