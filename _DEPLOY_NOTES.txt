BOTH FAULTS ARE THE SEEDED DATA. NEITHER IS A BUG.
================================================================================
1. TICKING ONE CC TICKS EVERYONE.

   components/VariationBuilder.js:421

       checked={cc.includes(c.email)}

   Correct code. Every seeded person carried the SAME address, so nine people
   were one identity as far as anything keyed on email was concerned. It is
   also what sent four copies of the daily email in pkg973 - where I deduped
   the sending and never followed the cause back to here.

   Fixed with plus-addressing, so each person has a genuinely distinct address
   that still lands in your one inbox:

       james+callum.reid@constructionliberation.com

   Staff, portal users, Site App operatives, CRM contacts and organisations
   all get their own. Checked they come out distinct.

2. NO CUSTOMER CONTACT ON THE VARIATION.

   customerContacts was seeded as an EMPTY ARRAY. VariationBuilder reads
   project.customerContacts for the recipient list AND for the greeting name,
   so there was nobody to send to and the greeting fell back to the company -
   "Hi Harbourline" rather than "Hi Jenna". That is also your Payment Claim
   finding, same cause.

   Two contacts per project now: the person who signs things off, and someone
   in accounts. Two so the CC list has a real choice in it and picking one is
   visibly different from picking both.


WHY NEITHER COULD EVER HAPPEN AT ROCK
================================================================================
Rock's people have their own addresses and its projects have real contacts.
Both faults need data that only a generator would produce. That is the
seeder's job - to exercise the states - and on these two it was producing a
shape the app cannot meaningfully handle.
