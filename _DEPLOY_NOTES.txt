pkg905 - The VAT estimate. Every month was wrong, not just August.
==================================================================

THREE FILES, none new.
  lib/xero.js
  pages/api/business-financials.js
  pages/business-financials/vat-refund.js


WHAT THE DIAGNOSTIC SHOWED
--------------------------
    lastError: purchaseBills HTTP 504: Gateway Time-out
    counts:    salesInvoices 196, salesCreditNotes 36,
               purchaseBills 0, purchaseCreditNotes 45

ZERO purchase bills loaded. Input VAT is the entire refund side, so the estimate
was sales VAT with no purchases.

And it was not only August. EVERY month in the table read "payable" with refund
0, while your filed history shows refunds of 17k to 50k every single month. The
whole estimate was wrong and had been since that sync at 00:28.


WHY IT TIMED OUT - the actual cause
-----------------------------------
The Xero query filtered by TYPE and not by DATE:

    where=Type=="ACCPAY"

So it asked for every bill Rock has ever had, page by page, and threw away
everything outside the month in JavaScript. 136 pages before Xero gave up.

Purchase bills is the biggest table you have, and it is the FOURTH of four
pulls, so it is the one that runs out of patience. Sales invoices - a smaller
table, pulled first - always completed. That is why output VAT looked fine and
input VAT was empty.


THREE FIXES
-----------
1. ASK XERO FOR THE PERIOD.

       Type=="ACCPAY" && Date>=DateTime(2026,08,01) && Date<=DateTime(2026,08,31)

   A fraction of the data, a fraction of the pages. This is the real fix; the
   other two are for when it still fails.

2. RETRY A TIMEOUT. 504, 503 and 429 are Xero being busy, not a refusal. Backs
   off 2, 4, 6 seconds and asks again, three times, before giving up.

3. REFUSE TO CACHE A PARTIAL PULL. The guard checked whether ANY data came back,
   not whether the pull FINISHED - and a half-dead fetch still returns months
   full of figures, so it passed and overwrote the good data.

   meta.lastError was sitting right there, already populated, already being
   stored alongside. Nothing looked at it.

   Now: if lastError is set, the previous figures are kept and the page says so
   in an amber banner - "These figures are not current. The sync did not finish:
   purchaseBills HTTP 504 ...".

   A VAT figure that is silently wrong is worse than one that is missing.
