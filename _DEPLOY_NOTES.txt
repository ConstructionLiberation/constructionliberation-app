MY MISS
================================================================================
996 routed every deletion of dashboard:cache through a guard that refuses to
delete a cache nothing can rebuild. I found the call sites with:

    redis\.del\('dashboard:cache'\)

Single quotes. This file uses DOUBLE quotes:

    try { await redis.del("dashboard:cache") } catch {}

Three times - saving, editing and deleting a WIP adjustment. So 996 protected
twenty-eight sites and left three, and a WIP adjustment is exactly the sort of
thing you would touch while sweeping. That is why it worked earlier and was
empty later.

Re-run with a pattern that accepts any quoting, single, double or backtick.
Three sites, one file, nothing else in the repo.

The lesson is the obvious one and I have written a version of it three times
today: a search that cannot tell you what it did not look at will find only
what you expected. A regex anchored on one quote style is exactly that.
