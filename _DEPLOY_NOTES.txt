PACKAGE 969 - "Forms" becomes "Site App"
================================================================================

One file. Complete replacement.

  pages/forms/index.js


WHAT CHANGED
================================================================================
Three places, not one. You asked for the login, but changing only that would
have left the header bar on every page still saying "Forms" two lines below a
heading saying "Site App".

  login heading     {brand} Forms      ->  {brand} Site App
  browser tab       {brand} Forms      ->  {brand} Site App
  header bar        Forms              ->  Site App

Both of the last two are inside Shell, so they change on EVERY page of the
Site App, not just the login.


WHAT I DID NOT CHANGE
================================================================================
"Project Forms" as the heading of the list you land on after login, and
"Missing Forms" in the CM menu. Those are about forms specifically and are
still the right word - the app is called the Site App, the things inside it
are still forms.


ONE THING THIS DOES NOT REACH, AND IT MATTERS ON A PHONE
================================================================================
public/site.webmanifest says:

    "name": "Rock Roofing",
    "short_name": "Rock Roofing"

That is what an operative sees under the icon when they add the Site App to
their home screen. Every customer's phone would install it as "Rock Roofing",
and on demo it already would.

It cannot be fixed the same way: it is a static JSON file, not a React
component, so it cannot read the tenant. The fix is to serve it from a route
that can - pages/api/site-webmanifest.js returning the JSON with companyName
and logoUrl substituted, and _document.js pointing the <link rel="manifest">
at it. That is a small package on its own; it was already on the outstanding
list and this is the second reason to do it.

Worth deciding whether it should read "Demo Site App" or just "Demo" on the
home screen - short_name is what actually shows under the icon, and there is
not much room.


SMOKE TEST
================================================================================
1. Demo Site App login - "Demo Site App" under the CL badge.
2. Log in and look at the dark bar at the top of any page - "Site App".
3. Rock - "Rock Roofing Site App". Tell your operatives, since the wording
   they have been looking at for months has changed.
