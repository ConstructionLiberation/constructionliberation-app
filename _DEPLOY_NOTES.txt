COMMITTED = what has been ordered on a project but not yet billed: each
approved / sent purchase order's value ex VAT, less the supplier bills that
have come in against it, never below zero.
BILLS ARE MATCHED TO POs BY THE PO NUMBER - a bill on the same project, from
the same Xero supplier, quoting the number in its reference, its bill number
or a line description ("APP-PO-0002", "APP PO 0002", "PO-0002" all match; a
longer number such as PO-00021 does not). To do this the project cost lines
now also keep each bill's reference, number and Xero contact - they fill in at
the next cost sync; nothing else about the costs changes.
"MARK AS FULLY BILLED" on a PO - for a bill that did not quote the PO number:
the PO is closed ("Billed - closed") and no longer counts as committed;
"Reopen" puts it back. A closed PO cannot be sent or changed.
WHERE YOU SEE IT:
  - Purchase Orders list: a "Committed" column (still committed, billed
    beneath); pick a project to see "Committed on this project"; a "Billed -
    closed" filter
  - a PO: "Billed so far" and "Still committed"
  - the project page: a "Committed (open POs)" card beside Total Spent
When Vinay confirms which Xero field Dext puts the PO number in, matching can be
narrowed to that field; until then reference, number and description are all
searched.

AFTER DEPLOYING: the next cost sync fills in the bill references - until then
bills already in Xero show as "not yet billed" against their POs.
