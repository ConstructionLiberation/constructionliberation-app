PACKAGE 997 - the empty Variations dropdowns
================================================================================

One file. Complete replacement. No re-seed.

  pages/api/dashboard.js


THE CAUSE
================================================================================
pages/variations.js loads its project list with:

    fetch('/api/dashboard?sync=true')

and dashboard.js treated sync=true as "skip the cache, go to Xero", where:

    if (!tokens) return res.status(401).json({ error: 'Not connected to Xero' })

So on a tenant with no accounting connection every caller asking for fresh
data got nothing at all - while the Budget Tracker, reading the SAME cache
without sync=true, was full. That is why the projects were missing from the
Variations tracker and the Variation Builder but present everywhere else.

The seeded data was never wrong: the projects carry status 'INPROGRESS',
which is exactly what the dropdown filters on.


THE FIX
================================================================================
The cache is now used when sync=true AND there is nothing to sync from. A
sync that cannot happen is not a reason to withhold data we already hold.

With tokens present nothing changes, so Rock still re-syncs on demand exactly
as before.

Same shape as 996, and worth naming as a pattern: three separate faults this
evening have been the app refusing to serve something it had, because the
thing that would normally refresh it was absent. On a single-tenant app with
Xero permanently connected, "no tokens" only ever meant "broken". With a
second tenant it means "not applicable", and those want opposite behaviour.

COMMERCIAL SCORECARD. Gross Margin reads from the Xero P&L; Weekly and
Monthly Commercial Tasks read tick history; Weekly Project Reports needs
labour allocated on the Gantt; Average Days Beyond Terms needs paid-on dates
that Xero's list endpoint omits - it says so on the card itself. Some of
those are seedable, some are structurally Xero. That is its own piece of
work, and after 995 it is also the page where hiding what does not apply is
the honest answer.
