pkg907 - WIP can be negative. It was floored at zero.
=====================================================

TWO FILES, neither new.
  lib/wipCalc.js
  pages/wip.js


WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
lib/wipCalc.js line 49:

    wipValue = Math.max(0, wipValue)

Any project whose adjustments exceeded its post-valuation costs reported 0.00.

J244 Waungron, from your screenshot:

    post-val costs        1,234.48
    adjustments          -4,500.00     (+6,500 materials, -11,000 labour)
    wip cost             -3,265.52

    WIP was (floored)         0.00     profit  3,265.52
    WIP now              -4,060.21     profit   -794.69

The 3,265.52 "profit" existed ONLY because the floor had moved the value while
leaving the cost alone. Profit is wipValue minus wipCost, so flooring the value
manufactured a profit exactly equal to the cost it was hiding.


WHY IT MATTERS
--------------
A negative WIP is not an error to be tidied away. It is a WRITE-DOWN - cost you
have decided you will not recover, or work over-valued in an earlier month being
pulled back. It is precisely the adjustment somebody has gone to the trouble of
entering.

Flooring it to zero hid the entry AND invented a profit from it. On a page that
feeds the monthly figures.


ALSO: NEGATIVE WIP NOW SHOWS RED
--------------------------------
pages/wip.js rendered the figure in green whatever the number, so a write-down
would have read as a gain at a glance.


CHECKED DOWNSTREAM
------------------
  pages/api/wip.js        totalWip += wipValue - negatives now correctly reduce
                          the total rather than being silently dropped
  cm-project-financials   invoicedIncWip = grossToDate + wipValue - a negative
                          now correctly reduces it
  the Zero button         adds -wipValue to cancel a project exactly; works
                          unchanged with a negative

Nothing assumed the value was positive.


CHECKS I RAN

  and without it
