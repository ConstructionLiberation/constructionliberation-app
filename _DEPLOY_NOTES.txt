(awaiting the CM, approved, sent, rejected and why) and "+ New purchase order",
made for a phone:
  - project: one of theirs - the delivery address fills in
  - supplier: search any Xero contact; a price-list supplier's products can be
    tapped in (quantity blank and red until typed), or "+ Add an item"
  - delivery: required date, address, Moffett and artics (Yes/No), access
    routes, site contact (search, or type anyone, with their mobile), order
    confirmations email (prefilled with the company default), notes
  - "Send for approval" - held until complete, saying what is still needed;
    then "Sent to <the CM> for approval"
PO APPROVALS (CMs) - the POs on their projects waiting for them; open one to
see the lines, VAT, totals and delivery details; Approve, or Reject with a
reason. An approved PO is numbered and goes to the office to send.

time (their sign-in had no token).
