THE PAGE MOVES
--------------
    was   /admin/design-customers
    now   /design/customers

It is a Design screen. Living under /admin made it feel like an Admin one, and
forced its back button to GUESS whether to return to Admin or to Design - by
role, which was right for one route in and wrong for the other, for everybody.

Moving it removes the question instead of answering it. Back now always means
Design, because that is where it is.

  - Removed from the Admin menu.
  - The Design portal's "Manage Customers" points at the new address.
  - /admin/design-customers STAYS, as a redirect. That address will be in
    bookmarks and old emails, and a 404 for somebody who has used the page for
    months is a worse outcome than one extra file.


WHY NOT REMOVE IT FROM DESIGN AND KEEP IT IN ADMIN
--------------------------------------------------
You suggested that and I would have advised against it, so it is worth writing
down.

The page allows admin AND management. But management cannot reach /admin at all -
AREA_ACCESS.admin is admin-only. So the Design portal's link is their ONLY route
in. Consolidating into Admin would have made you the one person able to add a
design customer.

Checked the other way too: AREA_ACCESS.design includes designer and
post-contract, so they can reach /design - but the page guards itself at admin
or management, and so does its API. So moving it does not widen access.
