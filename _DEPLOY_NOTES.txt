pkg888 - Pre-Contract can send variations to the customer
=========================================================

ONE FILE.  pages/variation-builder.js  - not new.

Nothing to do with tenancy.


WHAT WAS HAPPENING
------------------
Press "Raise & send to customer" on the Pre-Contract Variation Builder and the
page appeared to refresh. The variation reached the tracker as a draft, no send
modal opened, and no email went to anyone - not even the person who raised it.

It looked like a deliberate rule that Pre-Contract raises and Commercial sends.
It was not.


WHY
---
The page rendered the builder like this:

    {loading
      ? <div>Loading projects…</div>
      : <VariationBuilder projects={projects} onSaved={load} />}

So:

    press Raise and send  ->  save() writes the variation
                          ->  save() awaits onSaved(), which IS load()
                          ->  load() sets loading = true
                          ->  THE BUILDER IS SWAPPED OUT AND UNMOUNTS
                          ->  save() returns true, setSendOpen(true) runs on a
                              component that no longer exists
                          ->  load() finishes, the builder remounts empty

The "refresh" you saw was the component being torn down and rebuilt mid-save,
not a page reload.

pages/variations.js - the Commercial tracker - renders the SAME component
unconditionally. That is the only difference, and it is why the same button has
always worked there.

A component that unmounts itself by calling back into its own parent's loading
state. Nothing about that line looks wrong.


THE FIX
-------
The builder now renders unconditionally, exactly as Commercial does, and the
refresh happens underneath it. The spinner moved to the heading, where it cannot
tear anything down.

Pre-Contract now behaves identically to Commercial: raise, the send modal opens,
the email goes.


I CHECKED FOR THE SAME TRAP ELSEWHERE
-------------------------------------
Six other pages pass a reloading callback into a child component AND have a
loading conditional somewhere on the page:

    pages/variations.js                          VariationBuilder
    pages/operations/projects.js                 ProjectDetails
    pages/business-financials/invoice-finance.js SyncButton
    pages/business-financials/retentions-due.js  SyncButton
    pages/business-financials/invoices.js        SyncButton
    pages/business-financials/budgets.js         SyncButton

In every one, the component sits OUTSIDE the loading branch. variation-builder.js
was the only page where it sat inside. So this is fixed, not merely patched.


CHECKS I RAN
------------

- confirmed the builder is now rendered the same way on both pages
- confirmed it copes with an empty projects list on first render - the initial
  state is [] and the component does (projects || [])
- checked the six other candidate pages, as above
