WHAT I GOT WRONG FIRST, since it shaped this
--------------------------------------------
I told you a corrected bill in Xero would never be picked up, because
costs:labour and costs:materials only ever ADD. You pushed back: the app mirrors
Xero, and the totals should be the sum of what the app holds.

You were right and I was looking at the wrong store.

    costs:labour / costs:materials    running totals per job. READ BY NOTHING.
    costs:bills:<id> / costs:wages:<id>  per project, WITH their lines
         -> merged by lib/mergeCosts.js into
    costs:latest:<id>  the totals    costs:lines:<id>  the lines behind them

The figures you see come from the second set, where the total is summed from the
same records that supply the list. Exactly as you described it.

Four references to costs:labour existed in the whole codebase, and all four were
these two files reading their own total in order to add to it.


1. THE DEAD STORE IS GONE
-------------------------
Left in place it was worse than useless. A future reader finds a store called
costs:labour being carefully maintained and assumes it matters - and because it
only ever added, its numbers drift further from the real ones with every upload.
Anyone who started trusting it would be reading something wrong.

reset-costs.js still DELETES those keys. Deliberate: any values already stored
get cleared if it is ever run, rather than sitting in the database for ever
looking meaningful.

Checked that labourByJob and materialsByJob are still used in both files - they
feed the response summary, so nothing is orphaned.


2. deep-sync AND wip-sync MARKED AS UNREACHABLE
-----------------------------------------------
You asked whether deep-sync is used. It is not:

  - no entry in vercel.json
  - no button, no link anywhere - grepped across pages, components and lib
  - cron health has never recorded a heartbeat for it

Same for wip-sync.

KEPT, NOT DELETED, with a header at the top of each saying so plainly. deep-sync
pulls invoices, bills and wages from Xero; if the manual Bills upload path ever
gaps, that is the thing to reach for. Deleting working code you might want is
harder to undo than leaving a file alone, and they cost nothing sitting there
because they never run.

Both are still reachable by typing the URL, so they can be run deliberately.


A CONSEQUENCE WORTH KNOWING
---------------------------
Because deep-sync never runs, its mergeWindow function - which keeps an existing
line and discards a changed one with the same key - never executes either. So
the "a corrected bill is not picked up" problem I raised does not exist in
practice.

import-bills-bulk, which IS what your manual upload uses, REPLACES the lines per
project. So correcting a bill in Xero and re-uploading the spreadsheet does
update it. Your mental model was right throughout.
