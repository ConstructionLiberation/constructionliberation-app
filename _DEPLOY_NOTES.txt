pkg912 - WIP does not load for the bookkeeper
==============================================

REPLACE (2 files, both existing - no new files to create):

  pages/wip.js
  pages/api/wip.js

NOTE: same filename in two folders. Both must be replaced.


WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
/api/wip allowed post-contract, management and admin. Not accounts.

The PAGE guard already allowed accounts for the read-only embed - it says so
in a comment - and wip-pdf allowed accounts too. Only the endpoint that
serves the actual figures did not. That is the signature of a role list
copied from a commercial endpoint rather than chosen.

So the bookkeeper opened Bookkeeping > WIP, passed the page guard, and the
figures came back 403. The page swallowed it in a bare `catch {}` and
rendered an empty table, silently - which is why it looked like "WIP does
not work for accounts" rather than "accounts is not on the allowlist".


WHAT CHANGED
------------
1. /api/wip now allows accounts.
2. The page checks res.ok and shows why it is empty, in the embed as well as
   the full page. A blank frame inside somebody else's tab is the worst place
   for a silent failure.

wip-lock still does NOT allow accounts, deliberately. Reading the month is
not signing it off.


AFTER DEPLOYING - CHECK
-----------------------
Have the bookkeeper open Bookkeeping > WIP. Figures should load.
They should NOT be able to edit adjustments or sign the month off.
