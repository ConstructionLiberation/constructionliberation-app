OPTIONAL ENV: OPEN_METEO_API_KEY  - see "Licence" below. Works without it.

WHAT IT DOES
A "Weather" section on the Project Report, after Additional photos and before
Approval - in the editor, the read-only view and the PDF. One row per day:

  Day | Conditions | High / low | Rain (mm) | Wind / gusts (mph) | Flags

Flags: Rain (1 mm+), Heavy rain (10 mm+), High wind (gusts 30 mph+), Frost
(0 C or below). Indicative thresholds, printed under the table.

HOW
  1. The project's address -> its UK postcode (e.g. TF9 3SP).
  2. Postcode -> location: postcodes.io (free, no key).
  3. That location, the report's days -> daily weather: Open-Meteo, whose UK
     data comes from the Met Office's high-resolution model (~2 km grid).

WHICH DAYS
The same window the report's photos use: the day after the previous report for
the project, up to this report's date. A first report, or a gap of more than
two weeks, covers the 7 days to the report date. FINISHED DAYS ONLY - a report
dated today shows up to yesterday and fills in when reopened.

WHEN IT CHANGES
  Draft       fetched when the project or date is chosen and when the draft is
              reopened; "Refresh weather" to fetch again
  Complete    FROZEN at the weather recorded - a report that has gone to a
              client keeps the figures it was issued with
Saved on the report itself (report.weather). Editing a report from the Site
App keeps it (the save merges), though the Site App does not show it.

NO POSTCODE -> NO GUESS
If the project's address has no UK postcode the section says "No UK postcode
in the project address - add one to show the weather." A postcode the lookup
does not recognise says so too.

LICENCE
Open-Meteo's free service is for NON-COMMERCIAL use. For business use, take
an Open-Meteo paid plan and set its key as OPEN_METEO_API_KEY in Vercel; the
same calls then go to its customer service. Nothing else changes.

NOT YET

different address lookup - noted for the multi-tenant work.
