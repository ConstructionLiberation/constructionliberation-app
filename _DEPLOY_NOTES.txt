MATERIALS TAKE-OFFS > MATERIALS > "CREATE DRAFT POs..." now shows the POs it
could make FIRST - nothing is made until you choose:
  - a row per PO: tick, what it is for, the lines and the total
      PRICE-LIST SUPPLIER   its products (+ own lines allocated to it); its linked
                            Xero contact shown, changeable; not linked -> choose
                            its contact (or make it named and flagged, as before)
      YOUR CATEGORY         own lines allocated to a category of your own - e.g.
                            "Insulation" - choose a supplier for it
      NOT ALLOCATED         own lines not allocated - choose a supplier for them
  - the supplier: type to search your Xero contacts; choosing one ticks the row;
    a category or the not-allocated lines cannot be ticked until they have one
  - "Create N draft POs" makes only the ticked ones, each to its chosen supplier
    (a category's PO notes the category); Cancel makes nothing
  - items with no product chosen are listed; drafts made before are remembered
    and making more asks first
