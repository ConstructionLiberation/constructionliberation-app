A ⚙ beside the search box at the top of the CATEGORIES column lists every
category with an "In use" tick. Untick one and the whole category is hidden -
gone from the categories list, and its build-ups from the search and from the
take-off's "Item from build-ups" picker. "N categories hidden" shows; "Show
hidden categories in the list" brings them back, greyed and marked "(hidden)".
It works alongside hiding single build-ups (1137): showing a category again
restores what was showing - build-ups hidden on their own stay hidden.
Nothing is deleted; kept through renaming the category; stored apart.

The menu opens inside the column (with a Done button) rather than floating:
floating, it was clipped by the scrolling column and covered by the next one,
so its ticks could not be clicked - found in a real browser before shipping.
