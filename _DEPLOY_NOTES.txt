ACCESS
================================================================================
The Management tile already existed on the home page, shown only to the
management area (management and admin). Unchanged. Admin is included because
admin is above management everywhere else in the portal.

Every /api/management route checks the area on the server. Typing a URL as
anyone else gets nothing.

The tile also needs 'management' in the tenant's MODULES list, if the tenant
record has one. A record with no modules list shows everything.

HOW IT SAVES - AND WHY
================================================================================
Lists (goals, actions, outsourced services) save ONE ROW at a time, merged on
the server. Two directors editing different rows at once do not undo each
other.

Vision/Mission/Values and SWOT save whole. If someone else saved since you
opened the page, your save is REFUSED with their name, and your text stays in
the box. Nothing is overwritten silently.

Every refusal shows the server's message. None is turned into an empty page.

The API only accepts the fields defined in lib/mgmtDocs.js - an unknown field
is refused, not stored.

Keys: mgmt:vmv, mgmt:swot, mgmt:goals:quarterly, mgmt:goals:1y,
mgmt:goals:3y, mgmt:meeting-actions, mgmt:org:external. Business targets sit
in scorecard:targets under 'business'.


ORG CHART - A LIMIT TO KNOW
================================================================================
The portal records each person's job role but not who they report to. So the
chart is tiers (Directors / Managers / Commercial, Contracts & Estimating /
Site / Other), not lines between people. Anyone without a job role, or with
"Other", appears under Other - set it in Admin > Users and they move.
