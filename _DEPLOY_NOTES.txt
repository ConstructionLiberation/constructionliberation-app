TWO THINGS WORTH KNOWING
================================================================================
project-cashflow's gbp and gbpK were FUNCTION DECLARATIONS at the bottom of
the file, used by components three hundred lines above them. That works only
because function declarations hoist. Nothing was wrong with it, but it is why
a grep for "const gbp" found nothing in this file and my formatter count
missed both.

gbpK keeps its own rounding rather than going through money(): it prints
"$12k" and "$1.5k", and money() would put the grouping and decimals back in.
Only the symbol changed.


A THRESHOLD THAT IS NOT CURRENCY-AWARE, AND I HAVE NOT TOUCHED IT
================================================================================
sales.js has "Won >=£200K" as a headline stat and a matching column. The
LABEL now reads >=$200K on demo, but the threshold behind it is still the
number 200,000 applied to whatever currency the figures are in. 200,000 NZD
is not 200,000 GBP, so on a NZ tenant the label is honest about the number and
the number is arguably the wrong one.

Changing it means deciding what that band should be per customer, which is a
business question, not a formatting one. Flagged rather than guessed at.
