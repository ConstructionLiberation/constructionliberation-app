PROJECT REPORT - each drawing's row has a "VIEW / MARK-UP" button, and the

THE MARK-UP VIEWER (in the report AND the Design portal and handover documents,
which use the same viewer):
  - POLYLINE and POLYGON tools: click each point (the next segment follows the
    pointer); finish by clicking the first point, double-clicking, pressing
    Enter or "Finish shape"; Esc or "Cancel" abandons it
  - FILL on / off with its own FILL OPACITY - boxes, circles and polygons; line
    colour and opacity as before; colour, opacity and fill now also change the
    selected mark, and a shape being drawn
  - SNAP on / off - points snap to the DRAWING'S OWN line ends and corners (read
    from a PDF drawing - CAD exports have them; the button's tooltip says how many
    on the page) and to the points of your own marks, with a marker showing the
    snap. An image or scan has no lines to read, so it snaps to your marks only.
  - marks already made look exactly as before; nothing else in the viewer changes
THE REPORT PDF draws polylines, polygons and fills exactly as on screen.
