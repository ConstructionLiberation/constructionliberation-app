IT WAS NEVER THE DATES
================================================================================
991 widened deals to 20 months and spread decisions from 25% of a deal's
life. That was necessary and it did not fix the charts, because the problem
was never WHEN deals were decided - it was HOW FEW were.

    status = stage === 'stage_mc_secured' ? 'won' : (r() < 0.12 ? 'lost' : 'open')

38 decided deals out of 190. About thirteen per estimator, spread across
fifteen months. Strike rate and value secured are computed over a ROLLING SIX
MONTHS, so most windows contained nothing at all and there was simply no
point to plot. Dating them further back moved the gap; it could not close it.

Counted it rather than assuming: 19 won, 19 lost, 152 open.


TWO CHANGES, BOTH OF WHICH ARE ALSO MORE HONEST
================================================================================
1. STATUS DEPENDS ON AGE AND STAGE. A twenty-month-old deal still sitting
   open is not something a real pipeline contains. Late-stage deals over
   three months old mostly convert; early-stage deals over six months old
   quietly die; recent ones stay open.

2. THE STAGE DISTRIBUTION IS WEIGHTED. Picking uniformly from fourteen
   stages put only 4/14 of deals somewhere that could be won. Early stages
   still hold the most deals, but MC Secured and Negotiating now carry the
   volume a converting pipeline has.

Result, checked across three different seeds so it is not one lucky run:

    ~31 won, ~72 lost, ~87 open       strike rate 29-30%

Deliberately near the 25% target rather than comfortably above it. A first
pass came out at 39%, which made every card green in every month - and a
scorecard that is green everywhere demonstrates nothing about the scorecard.
Now some months beat the target and some miss.

Wins land in eleven separate months, so every rolling six-month window on the
chart has something in it - which is what actually draws the left-hand half.
