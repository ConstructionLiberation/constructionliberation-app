ALLOCATE TO is now a searchable box: click it and TYPE TO SHORTEN THE LIST
(suppliers on this estimate, your categories, price-list suppliers - still
grouped). If what you typed is not there, "+ Add '...'" makes it a new
category. Enter picks the first match (or adds the name). "— not allocated —"
at the top sets a line back. The list floats on the screen, so a short card
cannot clip it.
SEVERAL AT ONCE: a tick box on the left of each line, and a tick-all in each
table's header. With lines ticked, a banner - "N lines ticked - Allocate to:
[the same searchable box]" - sends every ticked line to one supplier or
category. Untick all clears the ticks.

tick-all ticked the rest. All 165 pages pass the browser check. Built from your
