PACKAGE 963 - money in CRM and Bookkeeping
================================================================================

Two files. Complete replacements.

  pages/crm.js
  pages/bookkeeping.js

These are the last two demo screens with real seeded data behind them - 190
deals in the CRM and a full bookkeeping dataset with a September backlog.


CRM - ELEVEN COMPONENTS, AND TWO THINGS THAT ARE NOT COMPONENTS
================================================================================
Four separate money formatters: two at module scope (money, money0) and two
more written again inside CompanyHistoryModal and ContactPanel. Nine
components now take the hook: ListView, BoardCard, BoardColumn,
EditableField, EntityTable, CRMPageInner, CompanyHistoryModal, ContactPanel,
AddProjectModal.

Two consumers are NOT components and cannot take a hook, so the formatter is
threaded in, exactly as resolveMergeFields was in 957:

  displayCell(deal, key)  ->  displayCell(deal, key, money)

  historyIcon(t)          ->  historyIcon(t, sym)

historyIcon is worth a word. It is a map of emoji for deal-history events -
note, call, stage change - and the icon for a VALUE CHANGE was a literal
pound sign. The one non-emoji glyph in the set. It would have sat there in a
demo history timeline looking exactly like the thing we spent the day
removing, and no grep for a formatter would ever have found it.


BOOKKEEPING - AND A CATCH THAT WOULD HAVE THROWN
================================================================================
Two components, two formatters: fmt on BookkeepingPage, fmtL on ReconPanel.

The reconciliation chart's Y axis prints its own k-suffix and needed
currencySymbol. I put it on BookkeepingPage and the axis is in ReconPanel -
so ReconPanel referenced a variable it had not declared. That compiles and
bundles perfectly and throws the moment the panel renders. The
declared-where-used check caught it; nothing else would have.


CHECKED
================================================================================
- Every identifier mapped to its enclosing component and matched against the
  declarations. Clean in both files.
- Every useFormat() is the first line of its component.
- Both compile and bundle for the browser with no duplicate symbols.
- One pound sign remains in crm.js, and it is deliberate: the `sym || '£'`
  fallback inside historyIcon.

WHAT IS LEFT, AND ONE THING TO DECIDE
================================================================================
Business Financials is NOT in this package, and it is bigger than it looks:
components/BizNav.js EXPORTS gbp and gbpK, and TWENTY pages import them by
name. Converting those to a hook means touching all twenty.

It is also not seeded. The module is switched on now, but the 48 keys behind
it are not there, so on Friday those pages render empty whatever currency
they are in. Seeding it is the decision, and the currency work should follow
that rather than lead it.

After that the remainder is genuinely cosmetic - scorecards, contracted
rates, procurement, the upload screens, and two PDF generators - none of
which have demo data behind them either.
