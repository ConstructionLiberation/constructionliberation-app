ESTIMATING > ESTIMATES - the list, newest first. + New estimate: search the CRM
pipeline and pick the deal (its name and customer come across), or start
without one. Click an estimate to carry on with it.

THE TAKE-OFF - everything is built here, in the order the quote will print:
  - Quote ref; PROFIT % and MCD % for the WHOLE quote; labour factor (raises
    all labour, each rate in proportion); day rate (starts from the company
    setting). Profit starts blank and is flagged until set - never assumed.
  - Project details (project, location, contractor, reviewer, dates, credit
    score/limit, insured limit) - "Show project details".
  - + Section header (Roof area A...) - with its subtotal.
  - + Item from build-ups (category / search) -> the build-up POPS OUT over
    the take-off. It is the item's OWN copy: changes stay on this estimate
    unless you "Save as template…" (name + category) into the library.
  - + Unique item - an empty build-up in the same pop-out.
  - Each item: description, qty, unit, rate (incl. profit and MCD), total,
    materials and labour (cost, for budgets), PROFIT % OVERRIDE (blank = the
    quote's), RATE ONLY (qty TBC, total "Rate only", below the line, not in
    the total).
  - Lump sums in a build-up (deliveries) are spread over the item's quantity.
  - Totals: total = materials + labour + profit & MCD; rate-only count; items
    still without a quantity.
  - Save - "Unsaved changes" shown until then; leaving warns. If someone else
    saved since you opened it, the save is refused (with who and when) rather
    than overwriting their work.
