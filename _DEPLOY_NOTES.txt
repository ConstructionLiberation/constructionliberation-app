VALUE PRICED - EXISTING CUSTOMERS
================================================================================
This is not a demo gap. customerType is read in about TEN places - the Sales
Dashboard, sales-crm and both scorecards - and NOTHING anywhere writes it.
There was no such field in the CRM. lib/crmDashboardAdapter.js had:

    customerType: null,

So the metric returned 0 on every tenant including Rock, against a 300,000
target, with a red dot beside it. And the Sales Dashboard filed every deal
under "Prospect" with dashes against Existing and New, because Prospect is
what a null falls through to.

A concept with ten consumers and no producer. Fixed properly rather than
seeded around:

  1. crmFieldSchema gains customer_type - a select offering Existing
     Customer / New Customer / Prospect, in the details group alongside Lead
     Source. It appears in Manage Fields and on every deal, editable, for
     every tenant.
  2. The adapter reads it, falling back to 'Prospect' - which is what the
     dashboard was already showing, so nothing moves for a deal nobody has
     set.
  3. The seeder sets a mix: roughly 45 existing, 64 new, 81 prospect out of
     190.

ROCK GETS THE FIELD TOO, blank until someone fills it in. Its deals will read
Prospect, which is what they effectively read today - the difference is that
now they CAN be set, and the metric will start moving.


THE EMPTY LEFT HALF OF THE CHARTS
================================================================================
Deals were created 0-13 months back, and a decision landed 45-98% through the
deal's life. So the earliest decision was about seven months ago and
everything before that was blank.

The scorecard charts a rolling TWELVE months, and each point is itself a
rolling SIX-month window - so it needs decisions up to eighteen months back
to draw its earliest points.

Deals now span 0-20 months and decisions 25-98% of the way through. Checked:
fifteen consecutive months carry decisions, from July 2025 to September 2026,
weighted to recent the way a real pipeline is.


WHAT WILL STILL LOOK ODD, AND IS CORRECT
================================================================================
Strike rate (MC Secured/Negotiating) reads 100%. The rolling window only
contains deals in those two stages that were decided, and in the seeded data
a deal that reaches MC Secured is marked won. So every decided deal in that
window was won.

Real enough - MC Secured largely does convert - but if you would rather it
showed a mix, the seeder needs some MC Secured deals marked lost. Say so and
it is two lines.
