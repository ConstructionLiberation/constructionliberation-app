COMMERCIAL > CASH FLOW - in a project's forecast window, under "Materials
forecasted on site", a new "ADD FROM PURCHASE ORDERS" button lists the
project's POs with something still committed. You choose: tick one and it is
added as a materials line - the still-committed amount, "PO number - supplier",
the PO's delivery date - with its payment term set right there like any other
materials line; untick to remove. A PO already in another forecast period is
shown with that period and cannot be ticked twice.
NO DOUBLE COUNTING AS BILLS ARRIVE: a PO line only ever forecasts what is
STILL COMMITTED on that PO now - never more than was entered, and nothing once
the PO is billed, closed, cancelled or deleted (that money is then an actual
cost). One rule (lib/poShared.js), used in every place a forecast materials
line becomes money: the project cash flow, and the business cash flow and
forecast P&L (Business Financials). If the committed figures cannot be read,
the saved figures are used as they are - never wiped.
