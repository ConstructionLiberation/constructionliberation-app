pkg909 - Cash flow: supersede drops SALES only, and the warning stands down on review
=====================================================================================

REPLACE (1 file, existing - no new files to create):

  pages/project-cashflow.js


CHANGE 1 - the fault found while specifying change 2
----------------------------------------------------
Superseding was all-or-nothing. When an application was raised, the whole
forecast for that period was skipped - including its LABOUR and MATERIALS.

An application is a sales certificate. It carries no spend figure at all,
because there is no spend event in it: operatives are paid weekly whatever is
certified, and material invoices fall due on their own terms.

So every time an application landed, that period's planned outflow silently
left the cash flow and the position read BETTER than it was. Same shape as the
WIP floor - no error, plausible arithmetic, missing input.

Now: the certificate replaces the forecast's INCOME only. Labour and materials
stay on their own dates and are adjusted by hand, which is the only place the
real figure exists.


CHANGE 2 - "CASH FLOW TO BE UPDATED" now clears when you update
---------------------------------------------------------------
Two triggers raise it. The first - "an application was raised since this
forecast was saved" - already cleared on save.

The second - "a period ended without an application" - did not, because the
period ending is a fact and re-saving does not change it. It now asks whether
anybody has LOOKED since: an updatedAt later than the period end counts as the
review and the flag stands down.

Opening the forecast and saving recomputes it against the latest application,
deducting what has actually been claimed. So the review is real work, not a
dismiss button.

A LATER period elapsing raises it again. Nothing is permanently silenced.


AFTER DEPLOYING - CHECK
-----------------------
1. A project with an application raised against a forecast period: the
   labour and materials bars for that period should still be in the cash
   flow. Before this they vanished.
2. Money Out for the affected weeks will go UP. That is the fix, not a
   regression - it was understated.
3. Open a forecast showing CASH FLOW TO BE UPDATED, save it, and the flag
   should clear without a page refresh.


NOT CHANGED, DELIBERATELY
-------------------------
Business Financials applies the same supersede rule in three more places
(business-financials/cashflow.js, monthly.js, reconciliation.js) and DROPS the
whole forecast there too. That is correct in those pages and must not be
"fixed" to match: they carry an actual-cost feed (bank:outstanding-bills,
wages, CIS), so once an application is raised the real cost has arrived and
counting the forecast spend as well WOULD double it.

The project cash flow has no actual-cost feed. That is the whole difference.

Worth knowing: the rule now exists in four places off two different sources
(appActuals here, dashboard cache latestAppEnd there). Extraction candidate,
but the copies are not identical in intent, so it is not a mechanical merge.
