LABOUR RATES
Pick a project. The schedule lists:
  - every ABOVE-THE-LINE contracted item (with its headings) - the contract;
    below-the-line items are not in it until instructed as a variation
  - every INSTRUCTED variation: one line per Variation Builder item (labour
    per unit = the item's labour total / its quantity), or one line at its
    labour lump sum for a variation not built in the builder
Struck lines are left out.

Columns:
  tick | Ref | Description | Qty | Unit |
  RATE (type the subbie rate) | TOTAL (rate x qty) | Match |
  LABOUR RATE | LABOUR TOTAL   (ours, from the contracted rates / variation)
  SAVING / (OVERSPEND)         (our labour total - their total; red when over)
Totals at the top: labour budget, subcontract rates entered, and the saving or
overspend on the items that have a rate.

  Match            copies our labour rate into the rate box (per line, or
                   "Match ticked" for every ticked line)
  Save rates       keeps typed rates on the project
  Create schedule  tick the items for ONE subbie, name it (e.g. the company),
                   Create - it appears in the Schedules list on the left

SCHEDULES (left)
"All labour items" shows how many are left to send. Each named schedule shows
its items and total; open it for the lines at the rates they were sent at,
our budget beside them, and:
  Download Excel / Download PDF   - ref, description, qty, unit, rate, total,
                                    grand total, company, project, schedule
                                    name and date. NEVER our labour rates or
                                    budget.
  Delete schedule                 - its items go back to "not sent"

Items in a schedule are GREYED on the main list, labelled "Sent: <name>", and
cannot be ticked or re-rated - so what is left to send out is clear. An item
can be in one schedule at a time; the server refuses a second.
