pkg921 - Admin navigation was three different lists
====================================================

REPLACE (3 files, all existing - no new files to create):

  components/AdminShell.js
  pages/admin.js
  pages/admin/templates.js

NOTE: pages/admin.js and pages/admin/templates.js are different files in
different folders. Both are here.


WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
The Admin nav bar was written out three times:

  pages/admin.js            9 tabs   (inline)
  pages/admin/templates.js  7 tabs   (inline, its own copy again)
  components/AdminShell.js  7 tabs   (everything else)

So the bar changed shape depending which Admin page you were standing on.
Email Notifications and Xero Connection existed only on Portal Users, and
leaving that page made them disappear with no way back except returning to
it. Your screenshot is the templates.js copy.

Nothing was broken in the sense of erroring. Three lists simply drifted,
which is the fault this codebase produces more than any other.


WHAT CHANGED
------------
One list, ADMIN_TABS, exported from AdminShell along with the bar itself as
an <AdminTabs> component. All three pages render it. The markup was
identical in all three places, which is how the drift went unnoticed.


WHO SEES WHAT - UNCHANGED FOR MANAGEMENT
-----------------------------------------
  admin       9 tabs, now on EVERY admin page (was 9 on Portal Users, 7
              elsewhere - that is the fix)
  management  6 tabs, exactly as before

Email Notifications and Xero Connection are marked admin-only, which
reproduces precisely who could reach them before: they only ever appeared on
Portal Users, and that page redirects anybody who is not an admin.

Widening either to management is a decision for you, not something I should
do while fixing a nav bar. Say the word and it is a one-line change.


AFTER DEPLOYING - CHECK
-----------------------
1. As an admin: open Admin, click Templates, Documents, RAMS Director. The
   bar should have the same nine tabs on every one, with the current page
   highlighted.
2. Email Notifications and Xero Connection should be reachable from any of
   them.
3. As a management user: six tabs, no Portal Users, no Email Notifications,
   no Xero Connection - same as today.
