I FIXED THE WRONG FIELD IN 987
================================================================================
987 set everIn1stContact and firstContactDate on the deal, because that is
what the filter in pages/scorecard-crm.js reads.

lib/crmDashboardAdapter.js OVERRIDES both:

    firstContactDate: (milestone && milestone.projectInDate) || null,
    everIn1stContact: !!(milestone && milestone.everInProjectIn),

It never looks at the deal's own values. They are computed from
lib/crmMilestones.js. Reading the consumer was not enough - the consumer had
a consumer, and I stopped one layer short.

crmMilestones takes projectInDate from fields.created, which the seeder never
set. The page then drops any deal without one:

    if (!d.firstContactDate) return false

everInProjectIn was ALREADY true, because the generated history moves OUT of
Project In and crmMilestones counts that as having been there. Which is why
the metric read 0 rather than looking obviously broken - half the condition
was satisfied.

fields.created is now the day the deal was created, matching addedAt and the
first entry in its history.


HOW MANY WILL ACTUALLY COUNT, AND WHY NOT ALL 190
================================================================================
CRM_LIVE_FROM is 2026-08-16 - the day the CRM started recording its own stage
moves. A deal created before that is "historical" and judged by a stand-in
rule: created within twelve months AND still sitting in one of four research
stages (Project In, 1st Contact, Calls x 3, TBF).

So roughly the deals in those four stages, created in the last year, will
count - order of fifty rather than a hundred and ninety. That is the rule
working correctly, not a shortfall: it mirrors exactly what Rock sees, where
there is no history before the webhook went live.

I have NOT fabricated a "moved into Project In" event to bypass it.
crmMilestones says outright that creating a project IS adding it to Project
In and there is no separate move to record - so inventing one would put an
event in the history that the real app never writes.
