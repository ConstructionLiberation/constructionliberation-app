WHY YOUR PO WAS NOT SENT: approving in the Site App only marked it approved -
sending was left to the office (the portal), and the Site App had no Send
button. Now the CM sends from the Site App:
  - a waiting PO: "APPROVE AND SEND" (approved, numbered and emailed to the
    supplier at once), "Approve only" (left for the office), or Reject
  - an approved PO not yet sent (like PO-0002): "SEND TO SUPPLIER" (asks to
    confirm, showing the address)
The CM's email is the SAME as the office's - one shared sender now (lib/
poSend.js): the company template, the PO with delivery details, lines, VAT
and totals, its attachments, the view / print link, reply-to the order
confirmations address. If there is no supplier email, the PO is still
approved and the screen says why it could not be sent.
THE HEADING now shows the supplier's name (the price-list name where there is
one), with "Xero contact: ..." and "Sent to: <address>" beneath. Your PO-0002
showed "sales@fixfast.com" as its heading because that is the NAME of the Xero
contact Fixfast is linked to - worth checking that contact in Xero (Dext
sometimes creates contacts named after an email) and renaming or merging it.

Checked through the real route: approve and send -> sent, the supplier's
email complete (message, PO, delivery, line, VAT and total, link, reply-to the
confirmations address), the operative told; approve only -> approved, then
Send -> sent, a second send refused; someone other than the CM refused; no
supplier email -> approved, with the reason. The office's sending proved
unchanged (template, attachments, sending after approval and the supplier
email tests identical before and after). In a REAL browser (Chromium, phone
size): Edit / Approve and send / Approve only / Reject; "approved and sent to
