WHAT WAS WRONG, AND WHY
========================

MARGINS SWUNG FROM -50% TO +71%. The page computes
(invoiced - costs) / invoiced. Costs were derived from certifiedGross, which
lags invoiced by a random 0-12%, so the division was against a different
figure from the one the margin was set on. Costs now derive from the same
number the page divides by. Measured after the change: average 26.5%, range
12% to 38%.

WIP WAS STILL EMPTY, AND THE WINDOW IS THE REASON. computeProjectWip takes
costs where date > valuationDate AND date <= monthEnd. With a valuation day of
28 that is a THREE DAY window - the 29th, 30th and 31st. My "post-valuation"
costs were dated within the last 26 days, which put them in the current month
and outside every closed month's window. Now a few lines land on the 29th-31st
of each of the last six months, so every selectable month has WIP. 16 of 30
projects have WIP in the August window.

RETENTION SHOWED 60 ROWS FOR 30 PROJECTS. The tracker merges retention:entries
with project-derived rows by project name. Mine said "Vogel Street Apartments"
where the project row says "J101-Vogel Street Apartments", so they did not
match and both appeared. Now matched exactly.

TOO MANY OVERDUE INVOICES. 46% of live invoices unpaid put nearly everything
in the debtor book. Now 25 overdue out of 206, about 12%, still spread across
all four ageing buckets.
