A NEW LUMP SUM or LUMP SPREAD line starts with NO cost category ("— choose —")
- before, a lump spread became Delivery and a lump sum Materials on their own.
Until one is chosen:
  - the category box is RED
  - the line is NOT IN THE RATE - "Scaffold: choose a cost category"
  - so the item counts as not fully costed, and the quote cannot be issued
CHANGING A LINE'S TYPE now sets its category: to labour -> Labour; to material
-> Materials; to lump sum / lump spread -> cleared, to be chosen. New material
and labour lines still get Materials / Labour automatically. Existing lump
lines that already have a category are unaffected.
