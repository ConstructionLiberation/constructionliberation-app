ESTIMATE > MATERIALS (between Take-off and Letter) - what to order, worked out
live from the take-off, grouped BY SUPPLIER with a cost per supplier:
  Product (where it is used) | Code | Needed | Estimate waste | Order waste %
  | To order | Packs x selling unit | Pack price | Cost
  - Needed = item quantity x the line's quantity per unit (sizes included),
    added up across every item that uses the product.
  - Liquids and powders priced by coverage convert to litres / kg: 1,000 m2 of
    primer at 5 m2/l = 200 l.
  - ORDER WASTE starts at the estimate's (product rules included; where a
    product is used at different wastes, weighted by use) and can be changed
    per product - e.g. 10% priced, 5% ordered. Blank = the estimate's. Saved
    with the estimate.
  - Packs are rounded UP to whole selling units; pack prices include supplier
    increases and this quote's adjustments.
  - Fixed-rate lines (not from the price list) are listed apart, to arrange
    separately. Rate-only items are left out (no quantity).
  Note: the take-off's "Budgets by supplier" is the PRICED quantity; the
  Materials total is WHOLE PACKS - so it is usually a little higher.
