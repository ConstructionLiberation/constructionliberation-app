pkg914 - "Application Total" renamed to "Gross Application Total"
==================================================================

REPLACE (2 files, both existing - no new files to create):

  pages/applications.js
  lib/applicationPdf.js


WHAT AND WHY
------------
The figure was always correct:

  applicationTotal = measuredToDate + variationsToDate + materialsOnSite

On J178 that is 314,935.77 + 36,714.00 + 0.00 = 351,649.77, which matches
Gross on the certificate exactly. MCD and retention are applied BELOW it,
in the certificate block, and both tie:

  Gross                351,649.77
  less MCD @ 2.5%        8,791.24
  Sub-total            342,858.53
  less Retention @ 5%   17,142.93
  plus Released          8,571.46
  Total                334,287.06

The NAME was the problem. "Application Total" sits directly beneath two
retention release lines it does not include, so the column visibly does not
add up, and the only available conclusions are "the total is wrong" or "I
do not understand this table".

Renamed in BOTH places - the screen and the customer-facing PDF - so the
portal and the paperwork say the same thing. The label column on the PDF
already carries "1st Release Retention (released previously)", so the longer
label has room.

No calculation changed. Nothing else touched.


AFTER DEPLOYING - CHECK
-----------------------
1. Open J178 application 1. The summary row reads "Gross Application Total",
   still 351,649.77.
2. Download the PDF and confirm the same, and that the label is not clipped.


NOT DONE, WORTH A DECISION
--------------------------
"Proj. Final Account" on this page and "Gross AFA" on the retention tracker
are the SAME figure under two names - both gross, both before MCD. The
tracker then shows "Final Account" for the figure AFTER MCD. So "Final
Account" means two different things depending which page you are on.

Renaming is easy; deciding what it should be called on a document going to
a customer is yours.
