COMMERCIAL > CASH FLOW - in a project's forecast window, beside "Add from
purchase orders", "ADD FROM COSTS REMAINING" lists the project's cost-remaining
lines (entered by the CM under Forecast to complete on the project page). Tick
one to put it in that period - its date and payment term set right there like
any other line; untick to remove. A line already in another forecast period is
shown with that period and cannot be ticked twice.
STAYS LINKED: a ticked line never forecasts more than the CM's CURRENT figure for
it - lower it on the project page and the cash flow follows; remove it and it
forecasts nothing. (If the costs remaining cannot be read, the saved figure is
used - never wiped.)
LABOUR IS LABOUR: a cost-remaining line in the labour category goes to LABOUR
out in the project cash flow, and to labour in the business cash flow and
forecast P&L - not materials; it is left out of the window's materials total
(shown beside it as labour), so it does not use up the materials budget.
Materials, plant and other go to materials, as before.
The same linked-line rule now covers both kinds of ticked line (POs and costs
remaining), in every place a forecast line becomes money.
