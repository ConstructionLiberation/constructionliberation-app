PACKAGE 1213 - Site App: CMs edit purchase orders before they are sent

Three files. Complete replacements. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1212.

  pages/api/site-po.js
  pages/forms/purchase-orders.js
  pages/forms/cm/po-approvals.js

A project's Contracts Manager can now EDIT a purchase order in the Site App
until it is sent to the supplier:
  - in PO Approvals, a waiting PO has "Edit" beside Approve and Reject: the
    same form as raising one, filled in - supplier, lines, quantities, prices,
    delivery details; the project stays as raised. "Save changes", then approve
    or reject.
  - an APPROVED PO the office has not yet sent stays editable - listed under
    "Approved - not yet sent" in PO Approvals; its Deliveries entry follows the
    changes.
  - once SENT it is fixed (as in the portal).
The edited PO must still be complete (the same full check), and every change
is recorded in its history with the CM's name. Only that project's CM can
edit - not the operative who raised it, nor anyone else.

Checked through the real route: Will (the CM) edited Dave's waiting PO
(quantity changed, a line added) - still awaiting, "Changed by the CM by Will
Reid"; Dave and Ali refused; an incomplete edit refused; after approval an
edit saved and Deliveries showed the new lines, the PO listed as approved -
not yet sent; once the office sent it, editing refused and Edit gone. In a
REAL browser (Chromium, phone size): the "Approved - not yet sent" section;
Edit beside Approve / Reject; the form filled in with the project fixed;
quantity changed, saved with the CM's token, back to the PO to approve. All
server routes compile; all pages pass the browser check. Built from your
