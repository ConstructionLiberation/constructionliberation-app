ON THE PROJECT PAGE (from Project Financials):
  - a FORECAST MARGIN card beside Current Margin - the same colours (25% / 21%)
  - a FORECAST TO COMPLETE section under Purchase orders:
      Costs to date + Committed (open POs) + Costs remaining = Forecast cost,
      against the AFA -> the forecast margin in pounds and per cent
  - COSTS REMAINING are entered by the CM as lines - description, category
    (labour, materials, plant, other), amount - "+ Add line", "Save costs
    remaining"; who updated them and when is shown.

arrive (cost moves from committed to costs to date) and as the CM revises the
costs remaining. The AFA is the same figure as the AFA card - one shared rule
(latest sent application, else an override, else contract value plus
instructed variations).
Who can enter costs remaining: post-contract, management, admin.
