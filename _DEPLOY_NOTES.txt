CLICK A PRODUCT TO ADD IT - the whole product row is the button (the Add
buttons are gone); the new line's quantity box takes the cursor.
QUANTITIES TYPED BY HAND - a product or free line is added with its quantity
BLANK and RED until typed. A PO cannot be raised until every line has a
quantity: the button is held back and says what is missing ("To raise it:
quantities (1), the Moffett question..."), and the server refuses it too
("Line 2 (Primer) has no quantity") - a line is never quietly dropped. An
approved PO changed to a blank quantity is refused the same way.
DRAFTS - "Save as draft" on a new PO; a draft may be incomplete (no supplier,
blank quantities, questions unanswered). It has NO number yet, is not
approved, not in Deliveries, cannot be sent, and its link does not open. In
the list under "Drafts", shown as "Draft". Reopen it: "Save draft", "Raise
purchase order" (it then gets its number and goes into Deliveries), or
"Delete draft" (only drafts can be deleted - a raised PO is cancelled).
Documents can be attached to a draft.

Checked through the real routes: a draft saved incomplete (no number, not in
Deliveries) and again; raising it refused while a quantity was blank, then
raised as APP-PO-0001 into Deliveries; a new PO with a blank quantity refused
by name, and an approved PO changed to one; a draft not sendable, its link
not opening; deleting refused for a raised PO, done for a draft. In a REAL
browser (Chromium): the Drafts filter; no Add buttons - a click on the product
added it with a blank, red, focused quantity; Raise held with what is
missing; Save as draft sent with the quantity blank; typing 12 cleared the red
and, with the questions answered, enabled Raise. All server routes compile;
all pages pass the browser check. Built from your deployed packages
(1022-1200, verified against their zips).
