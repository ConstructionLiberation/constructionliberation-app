PACKAGE 1090 - Estimates: project and customer details LIVE from the CRM

Three files. Complete replacements. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1089.

  lib/estimate.js
  pages/api/estimating/estimates.js
  pages/estimating/estimate/[id].js        (folder literally named [id])

Before: an estimate COPIED the deal's name and customer once, when created -
a later change in the CRM never reached it, and the letter would have printed
stale details.

Now a linked estimate stores only the deal's id. Its project and customer
details are read LIVE from the CRM deal every time it opens:
  Project, Site location, Customer, Customer address, Contact, Job role,
  Email, Phone, Tender return date, Start on site, Estimator, Credit score,
  Credit limit, Insured credit limit.
  - On the take-off they are shown READ-ONLY, marked live, with "Edit them in
    the CRM" - one place to maintain them. The letter (next) reads the same.
  - The Estimates list shows each deal's current name and customer.
  - A deal MERGED in the CRM is followed to the deal it became (the CRM's own
    merge rule), for the details and for the value a revision sets.
  - A deal deleted in the CRM is reported on the estimate, not shown blank.
  - Quote date and Reviewer stay the estimate's own.
  - ISSUING a revision keeps the CRM details as they were that day with the
    revision - an issued letter can always be reproduced exactly.
Not linked: the details are typed on the estimate, and "Link a CRM deal" links
it (then they come live from the deal). Estimates already created keep working
- their old copied header is simply not used once the deal is read live.

Checked:
  - the route: a linked estimate stores no copied details; edit the deal (name,
    site location, contact) -> the estimate and the list show the change; Rev A
    issued, then the contact changed -> Rev A keeps the old contact, the live
    estimate shows the new; deal merged -> followed; deal deleted -> reported;
    an unlinked estimate linked
  - the take-off in a simulated browser: live details read-only; a CRM edit
    shows on reopening; unlinked -> typed details + Link a CRM deal -> linked
    -> live
  - all server routes compile; all 164 pages pass the browser check
