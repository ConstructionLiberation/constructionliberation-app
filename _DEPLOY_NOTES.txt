WHAT I MISSED, AND WHY
================================================================================
Four pound characters, none of which my search could see:

    placeholder="£"           on the two inline money inputs, twice
    <Lbl>Budget Total (£)</Lbl>
    <Lbl>Buying Total (£)</Lbl>

I searched for '£' and `£ and currency: 'GBP' - quoted forms and formatter
declarations. A pound inside a JSX ATTRIBUTE or between JSX TAGS is neither.

The file now contains zero pound characters, checked by counting the
character itself rather than any pattern around it. All four use
currencySymbol from useFormat(), and both components declare it.
