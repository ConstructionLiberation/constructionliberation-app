WHY THESE FOUR PAGES FIRST
================================================================================
Everything before this was the portal, seen by the customer's own staff after
they have logged in. These four are seen by somebody else:

  reset-password    before anyone is logged in, like the login page
  rams-approve      a SUBCONTRACTOR following a link from an email
  instruct/[token]  a CUSTOMER instructing a variation from an email
  forms/index       the Site App home, on an operative's phone

A demo tenant sending a variation instruction that opens under Rock Roofing's
logo is the login-page problem from 19 September, one layer further out - and
this time it is not an internal screen, it is the thing a customer's customer
sees.

rams-approve was the worst of them. Seven references, including the BODY
COPY: "We've let the Rock Roofing team know", "contact your Rock Roofing
Contracts Manager", "This will be sent to Rock Roofing's Contracts Manager
and Director". All now the tenant's name.


MIDDLEWARE - AND THIS IS THE ONE THAT WOULD HAVE BITTEN
================================================================================
forms/index.js is the Site App, and /api/tenant-brand was NOT in the Site App
passthrough list in middleware.js.

Without the middleware change, the rewrite turns /api/tenant-brand into the
/forms PAGE. The fetch gets HTML back, r.json() throws, TenantProvider
catches it and carries on with empty defaults - so every operative, on ROCK,
would have opened the Site App to no logo and a heading reading "Forms"
instead of "Rock Roofing Forms". Silent, because the provider is deliberately
built not to break a page over branding.

The rule was already written down in the code nuances: a Site App route that
is not in that list does not exist. I read it after writing the page, which
is the wrong order, and it is the second time this session that the answer
was already in the documentation.
