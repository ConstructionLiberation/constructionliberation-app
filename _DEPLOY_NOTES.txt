WHAT CHANGED
================================================================================
1. /management opens straight on Vision, Mission & Values. The page of links
   is gone - it repeated the nav bar. Server-side redirect, no flash.

2. Text boxes grow to fit. SWOT points, and every multi-line cell in the goal
   trackers and Meeting Actions (goal, success measure, comments, problem,
   root cause, action, updates) grow as you type and shrink back. Loaded and
   pasted text sizes correctly too.

3. "Portal User Responsible" replaces Leader Responsible on the Quarterly,
   1-Year and 3-Year goals and on Meeting Actions: a dropdown of ACTIVE portal
   users. The goals also offer "All", as the workbook did. Meeting Actions do
   not - an action belongs to one person.

   Saves the user id AND the name. If someone leaves, the row still shows
   the name, marked "(no longer a portal user)", until it is reassigned.
   Anything typed by hand before this package shows as "(not a portal user)".

4. Meeting Actions: Team Member Responsible removed from the table. Any value
   already saved is kept in the data, just not shown.

5. Org chart downloads as a PNG - "Download chart (PNG)", top right. Titled
   with the company name and the date, includes outsourced services, drawn at
   double resolution for printing, named org-chart-YYYY-MM-DD.png.
   Outsourced services now also appear on the on-screen chart (dashed boxes)
   so the screen and the download match.

6. "Report app improvement" at the top right of the Management nav - the same
   shared component as every other portal nav.
