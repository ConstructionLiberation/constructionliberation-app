THE PROBLEM (J228) - a credit note on the retention account (612) is first
paired with the application it reverses (Xero allocation, or same reference
and the exact opposite amount). "Farmstead Drive - Retention adjustment" has
its own reference and no allocation, so nothing paired ("no candidate found")
and it fell back on its sign - £451.67 counted as RELEASED. Retention held
was right; Deducted and Released were both overstated by £451.67.
NOW - a retention credit note that cannot be paired REDUCES RETENTION
DEDUCTED. A credit note on 612 is never a release (releases are invoiced). In
the 612 lines window it shows as "adjustment" (amber) - "no candidate found -
reduces retention deducted". Credit notes that do pair work as before. A
credit note is also never counted as release money received.
STRAIGHT AWAY - the saved tracker figures are re-worked from each project's
saved 612 lines when read, so J228 is right as soon as this is deployed:
Deducted £2,451.67, Released £0.00, held unchanged. "Released and paid" needs
each document's payment state, so it corrects on the next Sync.

AFTER DEPLOYING: open the tracker - J228 should show the adjustment against
Deducted. Run a Sync when convenient to refresh "released and paid".
