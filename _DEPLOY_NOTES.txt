pkg902 - The Retention Tracker total now matches Business Financials
====================================================================

ONE FILE.  pages/retention.js  - not new.


THE 4K
------
The tracker totalled allEntries - what is on screen AFTER the status filter. And
the filter defaults to:

    useState(() => new Set(['live']))     Live Project only

Business Financials > Retentions Due totals everything. So the two disagreed by
exactly the retention sitting in Defects Liability, and neither page said why.

Retention in Defects Liability is money owed - that is precisely the stage where
it sits waiting for the first release. Leaving it out of a card headed
"Retention Outstanding" was wrong however the filter was set.

Worked through:

    BF (everything)                  13,000
    tracker BEFORE (live only)        8,000
    tracker AFTER  (everything)      13,000

    the gap was the defects row       4,000
    plus a complete row with a half unreleased   1,000

That last line is worth noting: a project marked Complete whose second half was
never released still holds money. BF counts it deliberately - there is a comment
there saying so - and the tracker did not.


THE FIX
-------
Retention Outstanding and Remaining to Claim now total the WHOLE register, so
they do not move when somebody ticks a filter.

The tracker's own code already knew this principle. Two lines below, the defects
and live COUNTS deliberately use everyEntry, with a comment: "how many projects
are live should not change because somebody ticked a filter." It was never
applied to the money.

The filtered figure is not lost. When the filter is hiding rows, the card shows
the on-screen total underneath in small grey - so the headline is the business
number and the filtered one is still there when that is what you want.

The old tooltip said "Ties to the totals row when all three status filters are
on", which was an admission rather than an explanation. Both tooltips rewritten
to say plainly that the figure covers every project and does not change with the
filters.
