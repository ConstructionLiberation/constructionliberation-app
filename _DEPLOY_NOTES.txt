PACKAGE 973 - four copies of the same email
================================================================================

One file. Complete replacement.

  lib/crmDailyActivityEmail.js


YOU WERE RIGHT ABOUT THE CAUSE
================================================================================
sendNoActivityEmail() builds its recipient list from portal users with the
role pre-contract or admin, and then LOOPS, sending one email per user.
Nothing dedupes by address.

On demo the seeder creates three pre-contract estimators - Callum Reid, Sofia
Marchetti, Dev Patel - all with email DEMO_EMAIL, plus your own admin account
on the same address. Four recipients, one inbox, four identical emails.
Exactly the four you saw.

It is not only a demo artefact. A real customer with a shared estimating@ or
accounts@ mailbox on two user records gets two copies of every one of these.


THE FIX, AND THE SHAPE BEHIND IT
================================================================================
Recipients are now deduped on the lowercased, trimmed address. Ran it against
the seeded shape: five matching users collapse to two distinct addresses, and
a differently-cased duplicate with a trailing space is caught too.

lib/ramsNotify.js ALREADY does this - it sends to a list it calls `uniq`. So
the rule exists in the codebase and simply was not applied here. Nine other
per-recipient loops have the same shape and no dedupe:

  pages/api/issue-notify.js
  pages/api/design-rfis.js
  pages/api/cron/design-rfi-outstanding.js
  pages/api/cron/design-rockdrawing-daily.js
  pages/api/cron/design-techsub-daily.js
  pages/api/cron/design-calculation-daily.js
  and the address lists in issue-send-customer and pre-start-notify-send

One rule, eleven places, one of them correct. The proper answer is the shared
sendEmail() helper that has been on the outstanding list since the start -
this is one more argument for it. I have fixed the one that is actually
firing rather than opening all eleven today.


WORTH DECIDING SEPARATELY
================================================================================
Even deduped, demo will email you this EVERY DAY: "142 projects with no
activity set". That is the app working correctly on seeded data where no
activity dates were ever set.

Three options, none of them code I would write without you choosing:
  - leave it, and accept one a day from demo
  - have the seeder set activity dates on most CRM deals, so the figure is
    small and the screen demonstrates the feature rather than shouting
  - stop the cron running for demo at all

The second is the best demo, because 142 of 190 with nothing set is not a
flattering screen for a prospect either.
