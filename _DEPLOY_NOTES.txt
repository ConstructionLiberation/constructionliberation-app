THE DECISION
================================================================================
Accounts (the bookkeeper's role) has the Commercial portal on the same terms
as post-contract: view, edit, send applications and variations, adjust WIP,
sign the month off, edit retention entries and set scorecard targets.

This reverses a deliberate earlier rule. The full WIP page refused Accounts
("no business editing adjustments or signing the month off") and the
retention entries were read-only to them. Both now allow it. The WIP and
Retention TABS inside Bookkeeping stay view-only - that is the tab, not the
person; the same user can edit on the Commercial pages.


HOW
================================================================================
lib/roles.js         'accounts' added to the commercial area. That alone puts
                     the tile on the home page and the column in Admin's
                     access table.

lib/portalAuth.js    NEW requireArea(req, res, area). Asks AREA_ACCESS instead
                     of carrying a role list. Same 401/403 as requireRole.

Ten routes           each carried its own ['post-contract','management',
                     'admin'] and would have refused Accounts on every
                     Commercial page. They now ask requireArea('commercial'),
                     so the next change to who has Commercial is one line.

retention            read: Commercial or Bookkeeping. write: Commercial.
targets              anyone with pre-contract, commercial or operations.
pages/wip.js         full page: Commercial. ?embed=1: Commercial or Bookkeeping.

Nobody else's access changes. Checked role by role:

  role           commercial  bookkeeping  targets
  accounts       yes (new)   yes          yes (new)
  post-contract  yes         no           yes
  pre-contract   no          no           yes
  designer       no          no           no
  management     yes         yes          yes
  admin          yes         yes          yes


A FAULT FIXED ON THE WAY
================================================================================
/api/task-definitions listed 'commercial' and 'bookkeeping' as ROLES. They are
areas; no user has either. So Accounts was refused its own Bookkeeping
checklist, and the Bookkeeping Weekly/Monthly Tasks pages silently fell back
to the built-in default list. If anyone customised the bookkeeping checklist,
the bookkeeper has never seen it. They will now.

The read now goes by the checklist's area. Side effect: post-contract can no
longer read the BOOKKEEPING checklist - they cannot open Bookkeeping, so
nothing they use reads it. Editing a checklist is still management/admin.


NOT CHANGED, STILL OPEN
================================================================================
The /api/project/[id] routes (the project page, WIP adjustments, settings,
comments, report) have NO role check at all - any signed-in user. Accounts
already worked there for that reason. Separate item.


SMOKE TEST - signed in as the bookkeeper
================================================================================
1. Home page shows the Commercial tile.
2. Every Commercial nav item opens with data: Project Financials, Outstanding
   Invoices, Retention, Variations, Contracted Rates, Applications,
   Application Calendar, WIP, Cash Flow, Weekly/Monthly Tasks, Commercial
   Scorecard (targets visible).
3. WIP full page opens (not bounced home). Add an adjustment, save, reload.
4. Retention: edit a manual entry on the Commercial page, save, reload.
5. Open an application, generate the PDF. (Do not send unless intended.)
6. Bookkeeping > Weekly Tasks: shows the customised checklist if one exists.
7. Bookkeeping > WIP and Retention tabs: still view-only.

Then as a post-contract user: Commercial behaves exactly as before.
