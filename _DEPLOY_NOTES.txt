TWO NEW METRICS
  Value of work priced - existing customers      (under Value of work priced)
  Value of work secured - existing customers     (under Value of work secured)

EXISTING CUSTOMER = an organisation we had already won a DIFFERENT project for,
before the date in question. Worked out from the deals - nothing to fill in.
  - Organisation matched by name, ignoring case and spacing. A deal with no
    organisation is never "existing".
  - "Before" means strictly before - a win on the same day does not count.
  - The deal itself does not count: repricing a job already won is the same
    job, not new work from an existing customer.

Same months, same last-full-month rule and same totals as the all-customer
cards, filtered.

NOT the CRM's "Customer type" field, which the estimator scorecard's
"Value priced - existing customers" card reads. That field is set by hand and
blank on most deals, so that card reads close to zero. The two will therefore
differ. Switching the estimator card to this rule is a small change if wanted.

IF YOU HAVE SAVED AN ORDER WITH THE GEAR, the two new cards appear after your
saved ones - move them up in the gear.


CARD COLOURS - Business Scorecard
In the gear, each metric now has a ◐ button. Click it for a row of eight light
tints - blue, green, amber, rose, purple, teal, orange, grey - or x for plain
white. A coloured card gets the tint as its background, a matching border and
a stronger bar down its left edge; its row in the trend table gets the same
tint on the metric name. The tints are fixed and pale on purpose, so a card
can never become dark or hard to read.

Saved with the scorecard's layout, for everyone. Reorder and hide keep the
colours. "Reset to default" clears colours too.

Colours are offered on the Business Scorecard only, as asked. The other
scorecards share the same gear, so adding them there is small.
