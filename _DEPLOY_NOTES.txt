WHAT IT WAS
===========
The grey prompt under each section heading on the Monthly Minutes named Rock
Roofing staff, hard-coded:

  Wins this month              "... (James / Carl)"
  General performance / KPIs   "... (Dori)"
  Up and coming news           "James / Carl / Nathan"
  Big focus for next month     "James / Carl / Nathan"

Every tenant saw them. On another company's portal that is four strangers
being told to write their minutes.

They now describe what the section is FOR, which is what a hint is meant to
do:

  Wins as a business, and anything worth learning from
  Main KPI data points against target
  Anything the team should know is coming
  The one or two things that matter most next month

Who fills a section in is a matter for whoever chairs the meeting, not grey
text nobody can edit.

Applies to Rock as well as demo, which is what you asked for. Nothing else on
the page changes and no stored minutes are touched.
