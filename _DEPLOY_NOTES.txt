PACKAGE 1210 - Purchase orders: the supplier email from a template, editable

Four files. Complete replacements. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1209.

  lib/poShared.js
  lib/purchaseOrders.js
  pages/api/purchase-orders.js
  pages/operations/purchasing/po/[id].js     (folder literally named [id])

"Send to supplier" now opens a proper email - To, SUBJECT and MESSAGE -
prefilled from the COMPANY EMAIL TEMPLATE with the PO's details, and editable
before each send. The purchase order itself - delivery details, lines, VAT and
totals, attachments, the view/print link - always follows the message
automatically, so a PO can never go out without them.
"EDIT TEMPLATE" (admins, in the send box): the subject and message, with
buttons that put a field in at the cursor - PO number, Supplier, Project, Job
number, Delivery required, Raised by, Company name, Order confirmations to.
"Reset to standard". Until edited, a neutral standard template is used
(nothing company-specific is stored by default). Saving it does not touch the
PO numbering.

NOTE: nothing is written to Xero yet - that is stage 3, waiting on the Xero
write permissions. App POs are in the app and in Deliveries only.

Checked through the real route: sending with nothing typed -> the standard
template filled in ("Purchase order APP-PO-0001 - Rock Roofing Ltd - J228",
"Dear HSS Hire, ..."); a company template saved by an admin -> used ("PO
APP-PO-0002 for J228", "needed 4 November 2026"); an edited subject and
message -> sent as typed; the PO details following every time; numbering
untouched. In a REAL browser (Chromium): the send box prefilled; Edit template
-> Delivery required put in at the cursor -> saved -> the email refilled; the
edited subject sent. All server routes compile; all pages pass the browser
check. Built from your deployed packages (1022-1209, verified against their
zips).
