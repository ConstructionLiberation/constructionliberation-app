PURCHASING > BILLS TO CHECK - every supplier bill in Xero since a start date
(default: a fortnight before your first PO; deleted and voided bills left out),
against your purchase orders. "REFRESH FROM XERO" reads them. Tabs, with counts:
  - OVER THE PO   the PO's bills all together, ex VAT, more than its value -
                  showing the PO value, billed and how much over (tolerance in
                  Settings: 2% or GBP 5, whichever is larger, to begin with)
  - NO PO NUMBER  from a supplier you raise POs with, but no PO found -
                  "LINK TO PO" (that supplier's POs listed first) or
                  "NO PO NEEDED" (with the reason)
  - PO CANCELLED  quoting a PO that was cancelled
  - MATCHED       the PO number on the bill (or linked by hand) - "Unlink" for one
                  linked by hand
  - NO PO NEEDED  with who said so and why - "Undo"
Click a bill for its lines and due date. Bills from suppliers never on a PO
(utilities, say) are counted but not shown.
ONE MATCHING RULE: the same as committed costs - the PO number in the bill's
reference, number or a line's description, from the same supplier (PO-0002 is not
PO-00021). A BILL LINKED BY HAND COUNTS AGAINST ITS PO IN COMMITTED COSTS TOO, and
only against that PO - so Bills to Check, the PO list, the project page and the
cash flow agree. When Vinay confirms which Xero field carries the PO number, the
rule is one line to change.
