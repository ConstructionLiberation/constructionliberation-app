ARCHIVED WHEN THE DEAL IS LOST
  An estimate whose CRM deal is LOST is archived - worked out live from the
  deal each time, so there is nothing to keep in step: reopen the deal in the
  CRM and the estimate is active again.
  - Estimates list: Active / Archived tabs; archived rows show the lost date.
  - The take-off: "Archived - the CRM deal was lost on 12 Oct 2026 (Price)",
    and the whole estimate is read-only.
  - The server refuses to save, issue or re-link an archived estimate, and
    refuses to link any estimate to a lost deal.

DELETE - ADMINS ONLY
  Admins: Delete on each row of the list and on the take-off (with a
  confirmation). The estimate's issued revisions are deleted with it.
  Everyone else: no Delete button, and "Only an admin can delete an estimate -
  ask James McVeigh, Carl Pryce." (the active admins, live from the portal's
  users). Enforced by the server, not just hidden on the page.
