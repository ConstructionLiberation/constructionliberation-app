THE CHANGE
================================================================================
    RETENTION OWED = invoiced (net) x retention %

was applied for x retention %.

invoicedNet where it exists, else invoiced - the same precedence the tracker's
own Invoiced column uses, so the two cannot disagree for the same row.

Recorded in lib/retentionCalc.js as a dated AMENDMENT rather than a
correction. The original paragraph is left on the record: it explains the
worked examples above it (J190 7,845.91 against 7,993, J197 6,914.35 against
6,435) and why the tracker and Business Financials once disagreed. Rewriting
it would make that history unreadable for whoever picks this up next.

The reason is in the file: retention is deducted on INVOICES, so an owed
total can only be reconciled against a 612 deducted total computed on the
same base. Owed on applied for and deducted on invoiced can only agree by
coincidence - which is exactly the coincidence that hid the original fault.


WHAT WILL VISIBLY MOVE ON ROCK
================================================================================
Every project where applied for and invoiced differ. Specifically:

  - the Retention Owed column and its total
  - Outstanding Retention on the summary card, which is owed less halves
    released
  - the 612 panel's "Retention Owed ... is the source of truth. Deducted
    should match it." - which is the whole point of the change
  - anything downstream reading the re-exported calcRetentionOwed, including
    Business Financials

Where something is applied for and not yet invoiced, Retention Owed will now
read LOWER and catch up when the invoice is raised. That is the intended
behaviour - the column is measuring invoices now.

Anyone comparing against last month's figures will see a step change. Worth
saying so before somebody asks.


WHAT I HAVE NOT CHANGED, AND YOU SHOULD DECIDE
================================================================================
calcReleaseHalf still computes from the FINAL ACCOUNT, not invoiced. The file
is emphatic that retention held and a release half are different things -
a half is contractual and fixed, and does not track what has been billed.

So the two columns are now on different bases ON PURPOSE. I believe that is
right, and it is the sort of divergence this file exists to prevent, so it is
flagged rather than assumed. If releases should follow invoiced too, say so
and it is one more line.


SMOKE TEST
================================================================================
1. ROCK retention tracker: pick a project where Applied For and Invoiced
   differ. Retention Owed should now equal Invoiced x Ret %, to the penny.
2. The 612 panel on that project: Retention Owed against 612 Deducted. They
   should now be comparable - that is the reason for the change.
3. The totals row and the Outstanding Retention card will both have moved.
4. A project with nothing invoiced yet reads 0, where it previously showed
   retention on the application.
