COMMERCIAL > CONTRACTED RATES - for a project with no rates yet, beside the
upload, "BUILD CONTRACTED RATES FROM ESTIMATE": pick the estimate (search by
quote reference, title or customer; a negotiated project's own deal's
estimates first). Its LATEST ISSUED REVISION - the one the customer accepted,
never a revision still being edited - becomes the contracted rates, in the
same preview an uploaded sheet gives: check, edit, Save, then Lock as ever, so
the budgets, applications and cash flow work exactly as before.
  - one rate per take-off item: item number, description, quantity, unit (as
    printed - m², nr...), the selling rate as quoted, and materials and labour
    per unit before profit (the budgets) - the estimate's own figures,
    including its frozen revision prices and its own material rates
  - rate-only items come through as "Rate only"; items below the line go below
    the line; above or below follows where the item sits on the take-off
  - an estimate never issued is refused: "issue it from Estimating first"
  - saving records which estimate and revision the rates came from - shown as
    "Built from estimate Q-1042 Rev A" - the project's link to its estimate,
    which the materials take-offs and draft POs will build on next
