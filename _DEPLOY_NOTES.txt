THE TAKE-OFF TABLE
  Always shown: Description | Qty | Unit | Rate | Total
  Chosen with "⚙ Columns" (top right of the table) - remembered in this browser:
    Materials rate | Materials total | Labour rate | Labour total
    | Profit rate | Profit total | Profit % (this item) | Rate only
    Suppliers - one entry per supplier, showing its rate and total: what each
    item includes from that supplier. Only suppliers with a BUDGET on this
    estimate are offered (a supplier only on a rate-only item is not).
  All shown at first except the suppliers.
  Materials, labour and supplier figures are COST (before profit and MCD) -
  the budgets; profit is the profit in the rate.
  A TOTALS row (above the line) sums every total column shown.
