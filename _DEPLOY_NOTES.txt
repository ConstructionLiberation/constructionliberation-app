PACKAGE 935 - the diagnostic could not check itself
================================================================================

One file. Complete replacement.

  pages/api/admin/find-string.js


WHY
===
Three searches of Rock's database in a row reported exactly

    keysScanned: 500, truncated: false

and there was no way to tell whether 500 is Rock's real key count or the scan
stopping after one batch and calling it finished. A clean "found: 0" is
worthless if you do not know how much was looked at - and the leak hunt is
built entirely on clean "found: 0" results, including zztest's.

WHAT CHANGED
============
The response now carries:

  keysInDatabase   the database's OWN count, from DBSIZE
  coverage         keysScanned as a percentage of it
  complete         true only if the cursor came back to zero
  scanRounds       how many SCAN calls it took

`complete` is now honest: it is false if the hit limit stopped the scan, and
false if it ran out of rounds. Previously `truncated: false` was reported in
cases where the scan had not actually finished.

Round cap raised from 200 to 400.

Nothing else about the search changed. It still writes nothing, still returns
no values, still refuses searches under three characters.


HOW TO READ IT AFTER THIS
==========================
On Rock:

  /api/admin/find-string?q=ll:&keys=1&limit=50

  - keysInDatabase around 500 and coverage 100% -> the scan was always fine,
    Rock's database really does hold about 500 keys, and `ll:minutes` genuinely
    does not exist. The Lessons Learnt page has been rendering from the seed
    file all along, and the two lessons files in pkg934 MUST NOT be deployed
    until that is dealt with separately.

  - keysInDatabase far above 500 -> the scan was capping and reporting success.
    Every "found: 0" so far, including zztest's, needs re-running. Tell me and
    I will fix the loop properly rather than instrument it.

Either answer is worth having. The point of this package is that the question
stops being unanswerable.
