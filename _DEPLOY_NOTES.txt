pkg882 - Stop telling customers their link expired when it did not
==================================================================

ONE FILE.  pages/instruct/[token].js   (folder uses literal square brackets)

Deploy AFTER pkg881, which is the actual fix. This is the message.


WHY
---
Your screenshot showed:

    Please sign in again.
    This link may have expired. Please reply to the email it came from and we
    will send a new one.

The first line was the real error, from our own session check. The second was
the page's blanket explanation, shown for ANY failure.

So a customer hitting a fault at our end is told their link expired and asked to
request a replacement - which would have failed in exactly the same way. They
try twice, give up, and ring Joe.

A wrong explanation is worse than no explanation, because it sends people down a
road that cannot work.

Now the "reply for a new link" line only appears when the error really is about
the link - expired, invalid, not found. Anything else says plainly that the
problem is at our end and to reply so we can sort it.
