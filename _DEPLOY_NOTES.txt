PACKAGE 1097 - Build-up lines: a rate on every material and labour line; own
items; lump spread; a searchable item picker

Four files. Complete replacements. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1096.

  lib/buildups.js
  lib/materials.js
  lib/estimate.js
  components/BuildupEditor.js     (the Build-ups page AND the take-off pop-out)

LINE TYPES: material | labour | lump spread | comment
  - "Fixed rate" is gone. EVERY material and labour line takes a rate:
      a product (or labour item) - its rate, which can be typed over
      (CHANGED / Revert, as before);
      or an OWN ITEM - no product: type a label (e.g. "Risk") and a rate.
    Material lines add to materials cost; labour lines to labour cost. Waste
    and price increase apply to material lines.
  - Existing fixed-rate lines (the Tape, Solvent, Butts... from the converter)
    are read as material lines with their amount as the rate - they price
    EXACTLY as before. A build-up or an estimate item converts them the first
    time it is opened.
  - "Lump sum" is now called LUMP SPREAD (an amount per job, spread over the
    item's quantity). Upload files may say lump sum, lump spread or fixed rate.
  - Own items are listed on the Materials tab under "Not from the price list"
    and are not counted against any supplier.

THE ITEM PICKER is now a search box: type any part of the description,
supplier code, supplier or Ref and choose from what matches - from the line's
own list, or the whole price list / labour rates with "any". Its first option
is "Own item (no product)".
