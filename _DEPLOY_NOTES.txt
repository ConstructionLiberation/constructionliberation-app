A "VARIATIONS" TAB next to Estimates. It lists the variations priced here -
project, variation, total, and whether it is in the Variation Builder yet
("V03 - sent 16 Oct at £835.64 - changed since", or "not sent yet").
"+ ADD NEW VARIATION" - pick the project (the Variation Builder's project
list, hidden projects left out), say what the variation is, "Open the
take-off": the SAME take-off sheet as estimates - build-ups, rates, columns,
Excel, "+ Rate build-up from another quote" and the rest. Its header reads
"Variation for J228 Farmstead Drive".
"SEND TO VARIATION BUILDER" (top right) puts it into that project's
variations, as the builder writes them, as a DRAFT ready for sending:
  - numbered after the project's existing variations (V01, V02 -> V03)
  - one builder item per priced take-off item - quantity, unit, its materials
    and labour, and the mark-up on cost that gives exactly the take-off's
    total; items without a quantity are left out (and named)
  - materials / labour / profit totals for the tracker, final account,
    applications and cash flow, as builder-made variations already are
  - the sub-contract ref, and this variation's clarifications (or the
    builder's standard ones)
Sending again UPDATES the same variation ("Update V03 in the Variation
Builder") - unless it has been instructed, which the builder locks.
Variations stay off the Estimates list and do not touch the CRM. The
builder's standard clarifications now live in lib/variationDefaults.js,
shared by both.
