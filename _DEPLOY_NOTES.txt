WHAT I GOT WRONG IN pkg903
--------------------------
You said retention comes from the application, and I collapsed two different
things into one rule. They are not the same:

    retention HELD     accrues as you apply      applied for x ret%
    a release HALF     contractual, fixed        final account x ret% / 2

pkg903 made the halves half of the HELD figure. That would have made the portal
disagree with the certificates your customers receive, which is the wrong way
round - the certificate is the document that matters.

Your own codebase already says so, in pages/api/dashboard.js:

    "A half falling due at 70% applied for is still half of the WHOLE retention -
     which is what the application certificate releases, and the tracker was
     showing half of retention-to-date instead. On a 580k account at 5% MCD and
     3% retention that was 6,227 a half against the 8,265 the application
     actually claims."

lib/applications.js has always computed it this way. pkg903 would have moved
both pages away from it.


WHAT THIS DOES
--------------
    calcRetentionOwed    applied for x ret%              retention HELD
    calcReleaseHalf      retentionOnFinalAccount / 2     CONTRACTUAL half

retentionOnFinalAccount is computed on the project record as Gross AFA less MCD
times the retention percentage - the same base lib/applications.js uses, so the
portal and the certificate now agree. Both pages now carry that field onto their
rows; neither did before, so both would have fallen back to a less accurate
calculation.

Worked against your real rows:

    ref    held (applied)   half (final acct)   pkg903 would have shown
    J190      15,691.81         10,041.59              7,845.91
    J197      13,828.70          7,760.78              6,914.35
    J228       4,208.23          2,104.11              2,104.11
    J244       4,581.33         10,940.30              2,290.66

J228 is fully applied for, so the two bases agree - which is exactly how the
original fault hid.

J244 is the extreme: 21% applied for, so a contractual half of 10,940 against
2,291 of accrued retention.


A CONSEQUENCE, STATED PLAINLY
-----------------------------
Because the two sides use different bases ON PURPOSE, mid-contract a released
half can exceed what has accrued, and Outstanding can read lower than the
remaining halves suggest.

That is the real commercial position, not an error: you have claimed a
contractual half against retention not yet fully accrued. The two converge as
the job completes, because applied for converges on the final account. Written
into lib/retentionCalc.js so the next person does not "fix" it.
