pkg881 - The variation instruction link, for people who have no account
=======================================================================

TWO FILES, neither new.
  middleware.js          (repository root)
  lib/withTenant.js


WHAT YOU FOUND
--------------
You clicked an instruction link and were asked to sign in. You said you should
not have to. You are right, and there were TWO separate reasons - one of them
older than tenancy.


1. THE PAGE WAS NEVER PUBLIC
----------------------------
The link in the email is /instruct/<signed token>, and pages/instruct/[token].js
was NOT in middleware's open list. Nor was /api/variation-instruct, which that
page calls.

So a customer with no portal account - which is every customer - clicking that
button was bounced to /login, where they can do nothing.

THIS IS NOT NEW. It predates all of today's work. It went unnoticed because
everyone who clicks it while testing is already signed in, so the redirect never
fires for them.

That means it has been failing for real recipients. Worth asking Joe or Nathan
whether customers have ever said the Instruct button did not work - if they have,
this is why.

Both the page and its endpoint are now open, alongside the RAMS and In Query
links which were already there for exactly this reason.


2. A STALE COOKIE BLOCKED IT
----------------------------
Separately, with tenancy on, withTenant refused any request carrying a session
that did not match the resolved customer - including these token links.

That is wrong twice over: it asks a CUSTOMER to sign in to a portal they have no
account for, and it blocks the one thing the link exists to do.

These routes are authenticated by the TOKEN, not by a session, and the token
already carries its own customer and is checked against the resolved one inside
the handler. So a stale cookie is simply irrelevant. It is now ignored rather
than fatal, for:

    /instruct/                  /api/variation-instruct
    /api/inquery-review         /api/rams-token
    /api/rams-approvals         /api/download
    /go/


CHECKS I RAN
------------
- esbuild compile on both: 0 errors
- middleware still imports only next/server - it runs at the edge
- traced what the instruct PAGE calls and confirmed that endpoint is covered
  too. Opening the page alone would only have moved the failure one step later:
  the customer reaches the screen and then every button on it is refused.
- reviewed the full open list, printed in this package for the record


WHAT TO CHECK
-------------
1. Send a variation to yourself and open the instruction link in an INCOGNITO
   window - no session at all. That is what your customer actually experiences,
   and it is the only way to test it properly.
2. Instruct it. Confirm Project Financials picks up the AFA change.
3. Then open a link in your normal window, signed in. Should also work, with no
   sign-in prompt.
