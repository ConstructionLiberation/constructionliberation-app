PACKAGE 1177 - Delete the draft, not the estimate; a bin for deleted estimates

Four files. Complete replacements. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1176.

  pages/estimating/estimate/[id].js      (folder literally named [id])
  pages/estimating/estimates.js

TWO DELETES, CLEARLY APART (top right of the estimate):
  "Delete draft Rev B" - discards the draft revision only; the estimate goes
    back to its latest issued revision (as issued, read-only). Documents added
    to the draft are removed. ANYONE can do this. While editing an issued
    revision it reads "Discard edits to Rev A" and discards those edits.
  "Delete estimate" - the whole estimate, every revision. Never issued: ANYONE.
    Issued at least once: ADMINS only (others see who to ask). The confirm
    says it is the whole estimate and points to "Delete draft" when there is
    one.
A BIN - a deleted estimate goes to "Deleted estimates" (a tab on the Estimates
list, admins only) with its revisions and documents intact: Restore, or Delete
permanently. After 30 days it is removed for good.
LOST IN THE CRM -> ARCHIVED - unchanged: the estimate is archived, read-only,
until the deal is reopened.

NOTE: estimates deleted BEFORE this package were removed permanently - the bin
only catches deletes from now on.
