OPERATIONS > PURCHASING > SUPPLIERS - every supplier on the price list (with
its product count), linked to its supplier contact in Xero:
  - "Refresh from Xero" reads the company's Xero contacts (read-only - the
    contacts permission the app already has; no reconnect needed)
  - each unlinked supplier shows its closest Xero contacts: "Link to Fixfast
    Ltd" in one click, or "Choose..." to search
  - SEVERAL Xero contacts can be linked to one supplier (duplicates such as
    "Soprema" / "Soprema UK Ltd"); one is the MAIN contact, for POs
  - "We raise POs with them" (on by default - POs come from the price list)
FOR VINAY (the accounts role can open this page):
  - "Dext should publish as": Awaiting Approval for a PO supplier, else
    Awaiting Payment
  - "Set in Dext": he ticks it once the supplier's Dext rule matches; until
    then it shows "To change in Dext". Only accounts, management and admin can
    tick it. Changing whether we raise POs with a supplier un-ticks it, so it
    is set again.
  - filters: Not linked to Xero / To change in Dext, with counts
A company with no Xero connected gets the list and a "not connected" note -
not an error. Nothing is written to Xero.

AFTER DEPLOYING: open Operations > Purchasing > Suppliers, "Refresh from
Xero", then link each supplier. Send Vinay the page link
(/operations/purchasing/suppliers) for the Dext column.
