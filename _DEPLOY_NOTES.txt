AVERAGE VALUE OF SECURED PROJECT  (renamed from "...newly secured projects")
  Deals WON while at the Negotiating stage, with a value. Each month: that
  month's average. Card: the average over the period (total / count, not an
  average of monthly averages). A month with no wins is blank, not 0.

STRIKE RATE ON VALUE
  The estimator scorecard's strike rate (value), whole business: won value /
  decided value, rolling six months to each month end. Card: the latest month.
  ONE rule now (lib/precontractTotals.js) - the estimator page calls it too.

  This also FIXES the estimator page's window. It built month ends with
  toISOString(), which is UTC: anywhere ahead of UTC (the UK in summer, NZ
  always) the "31st" became the 30th, so a deal decided on a month's last day
  fell outside the window. Its all-estimator strike rate can move slightly on
  months where that happened - it is now right. (The PER-PERSON strike rate
  cards still use the old window; same fix available if wanted.)

NEGOTIATING PIPELINE
  Open deals at Negotiating, total value, at each MONTH END. This month: as it
  stands today, by exactly the Negotiating page's rule.
  Past month ends are REBUILT from each deal's history - its stage and value
  at the end of that day, and whether it had been won or lost by then.
  Limit: a deal with no recorded history (Pipedrive era) is taken to have
  been at its current stage since it was created, and at its current value.
  Exact for everything recorded in the CRM.
  Card: the latest - "Today", or the last month end of a past range.

PAYLESS NOTICES
  Credit notes, counted EXACTLY as the Commercial Scorecard: live projects,
  dated by the credit note, your manual month adjustments applied. Card: the
  total for the period. The Commercial Scorecard's route now uses the same
  shared rule - checked by running it before and after on the same data: the
  whole response is identical.
