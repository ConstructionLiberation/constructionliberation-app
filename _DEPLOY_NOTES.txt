GROSS MARGIN %
================================================================================
Source: the Xero P&L that Business Financials reads (xero:pl-benchmark,
filled by Bookkeeping -> Sync Xero figures). No new Xero calls.

  Each month    that month's gross profit / that month's sales
                (sales - cost of sales) / sales. Trend line as the others.
  Headline      the margin for the financial year so far, first month to the
                last FULL month, labelled e.g. "FY2026 to date" with
                "Dec 25 - Sep 26, 10 months" underneath.
                Worked from the TOTALS, not an average of the monthly
                percentages - a big month counts for more than a quiet one.
  Current month blank. It is part-posted and would swing the line.
  No sales      blank, not 0%.

The rule is now ONE function (lib/plMargin.js), used by both the scorecard and
Business Financials, so the two can never show different margins. Business
Financials' figures are unchanged - same calculation, moved.


THE FILTER
================================================================================
Replaces the From/To dates. Options:

  Last 12 months                          (default)
  FY2026 (Dec 25 - Nov 26) - this year
  FY2025 (Dec 24 - Nov 25)
  ... one per year that has Xero figures

Pick a past year: the twelve months of that year, and the headline becomes
"FY2025 full year". Applies to every card.


FINANCIAL YEAR - NOW A SETTING
================================================================================
Business Financials has always assumed December. That is a fact about Rock
sitting in shared code; an April-start customer would have got figures cut on
the wrong year with nothing on screen to say so. The scorecard reads
fyStartMonth from the tenant record instead.

NOT CHANGED here: the Business Financials PAGES still assume December in
several places (summary cards, VAT refund, budgets, cash schedule). Correct
for Rock. Added to the outstanding list for a second customer.
