On CREATE NEW REVISION the portal compares the prices the latest revision went
out with against today's price list - every product and labour rate the quote
uses. If any have changed it asks:
  "The price list has changed since Rev A - Do you want to update the new quote
  with the rates from the latest price list?"
listing each change (e.g. "Alkorplus polythene VCL £248.00 -> £124.00") and the
quote total at Rev A's prices -> at today's, with:
  Update to the latest price list - the new revision uses today's rates
  Keep Rev A's prices - the new revision keeps the prices Rev A went out with
                        ("Use today's prices" can switch it later)
  Cancel - no new revision
If nothing has changed it simply creates the revision. This estimate's own
Materials-tab rates hold either way.
