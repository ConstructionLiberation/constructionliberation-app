RED IN THE CLARIFICATIONS TEMPLATE - in Edit template (and Admin > Quote
Documents) select PART of a sentence or ALL of it and "Make red" - for what may
not apply to every quote; "Clear red" removes it. Make / Clear red sit once
above the list and act on the clarification your selection is in.
ON A QUOTE'S CLARIFICATIONS PAGE the red shows red (no markers), with a warning
"N red parts - check each applies". Select the red (or click in it) and
"Clear red" to keep it as it is, or type over it - it turns black - or delete
the line. Like the letter, the quote CANNOT BE SENT while any red is left (the
server refuses too, also for Mark as sent).
FIX - TYPING OVER RED (letter and clarifications): the first key turned the red
black and the cursor lost its place, so the rest of the typing went in before
the first letter ("7:30am" came out ":30am...7"). The cursor now stays put.
