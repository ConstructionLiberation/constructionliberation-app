VAT ON BY DEFAULT - every PO is at the standard rate unless "No VAT" is
chosen (beside the totals on the PO), e.g. for a supplier that is not VAT
registered. The totals read Subtotal (ex VAT), VAT, Total - on the PO, in the
email, on the supplier's page; the list shows the total inc VAT with the ex
VAT figure beneath.
THE RATE - a company setting in PO settings (20% to start; another country's
rate, e.g. GST 15%, for other companies). Each PO KEEPS the rate it was raised
at - a later change does not alter it; a draft takes the rate in force when it
is raised. POs raised before this are treated as standard 20% (as the
supplier will invoice them), and the list works their VAT out from the PO.

Checked through the real routes: a GBP 1,000 PO -> VAT 200, total 1,200; No
VAT -> 0 and 1,000; the rate changed to 15% -> a new PO 150 / 1,150, the
earlier PO still 200 / 1,200; a draft saved at 20% and raised after the
change -> 15%; the email "Subtotal (ex VAT) 1,000.00 VAT 20% 200.00 Total
1,200.00"; the supplier's view of a No VAT PO; a PO from before VAT listed as
148.00 + 29.60 = 177.60. In a REAL browser (Chromium): the totals and the
20% / No VAT switch; the list's inc-VAT column; the VAT rate in PO settings.
