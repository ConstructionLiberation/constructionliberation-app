LABOUR SCHEDULE - a new tab on each estimate (after Materials). Exactly the
Contracted Rates > Labour Rates screen, off the estimate's take-off instead:
  - the take-off's labour: its sections as headings, each item WITH labour -
    quantity, unit, its description (with the build-up's), and OUR labour rate
    per unit (before profit) and total; below-the-line items under their own
    heading; materials-only items left out
  - type a subbie's rate (Match copies ours) -> their total, and the saving
  - tick lines -> Create schedule -> a named schedule for that subbie
  - each schedule downloads as EXCEL or PDF - their rates only, NEVER ours
Schedules are kept with the estimate (separately from the take-off). They are
made from the SAVED take-off - the tab says "save first" if there are unsaved
changes. Contracted Rates > Labour Rates works exactly as before.

Checked through the real route with the converted build-ups, price list and
labour rates: Mech Fixed SP on a 450 m² item -> "Roof area A", "1. Main roof
450 m² @ our 11.00 = 4,950.00"; a materials-only item left out; ABC Roofing's
rate 9.50 saved; schedule created (4,275.00); the Excel file - "Project:
Q-1042 - Market Drayton", 450 m² @ 9.50 = 4,275.00 and NO 11.00 / 4,950.00;
the PDF likewise. In a REAL browser (Chromium): the tab after Materials; Mech
fixed single ply 1000 m²; 9.50 typed -> 9,500.00 beside our 11.00 /
11,000.00, saving 1,500.00. All server routes compile; all 166 pages pass the
browser check. Built from your deployed packages (1022-1166, verified against
