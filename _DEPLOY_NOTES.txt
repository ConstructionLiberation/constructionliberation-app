WHAT HAPPENED
-------------
Tenancy is now ON, and every existing session is correctly rejected - sessions
issued before pkg861 carry no customer, and assuming they were Rock's would have
left the cross-tenant bypass open for the seven days they take to expire.

But the check runs on EVERY wrapped route, INCLUDING /api/portal-auth. So the
stale cookie blocked the very request needed to replace it:

    sign in  ->  refused  ->  still holding the same cookie  ->  refused

The only way out was clearing cookies by hand or an incognito window. That is
not something to ask eight people to work out on a Monday morning, and the
message did not tell them to.

My fault, and the kind that only appears the moment it affects everyone at once.


THE FIX
-------
The refusal now EXPIRES the cookie as well as returning 401:

    Set-Cookie: rr_portal_session=; Max-Age=0

The browser drops it, the retry carries no cookie, and the login proceeds
normally. One refusal, then a normal sign-in.

Message shortened to "Please sign in again." - the old wording explained a cause
nobody could act on.

- confirmed the cookie name and attributes match exactly how portal-auth.js
  clears it on logout - same Path, HttpOnly, SameSite, Secure, Max-Age=0. A
  clear that does not match the original attributes does not clear anything.
