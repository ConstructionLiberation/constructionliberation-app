PACKAGE 1068 - Report weather: postcodes not in the national file

Two files. Complete replacements. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1067.

  lib/siteWeather.js
  components/ReportWeather.js

THE FAULT
"Postcode ... was not recognised." The lookup only knew CURRENT postcodes.
Building sites are exactly where a postcode is too new to be in the national
file yet, or has been retired.

THE FIX - three tries, in order
  1. the postcode itself
  2. the retired-postcodes list, which keeps their location
  3. the postcode DISTRICT (TF9 3SP -> TF9) and its centre
The weather grid is about 2 km square, so a district centre gives effectively
the same week's weather. When the district is used, the note under the table
says so: "not yet in the postcode file - the centre of TF9 was used".

Only if even the district is unknown does the report refuse, and then it says
the postcode itself looks wrong: "Postcode ... could not be found, not even its
district (...). Check the postcode in the project's address."

Draft reports pick this up the next time they are opened, or with Refresh
weather.

Checked: a current postcode (found directly); a retired one (found on the
retired list); a new-build not yet listed (found by its district, noted); a
nonsense one (refused with the new message). The report page and route
bundle; all 158 pages pass the browser check.
