VALUE OF WORK PRICED
  Each month: every value change on every project in the month, added up.
  Blank -> 100k adds 100k. 20k -> 25k adds 5k. A price cut subtracts.
  A project repriced twice in a month counts both changes.
  Card: the total for the period - FY2026 to date by default.

VALUE OF WORK SECURED
  Each month: the value of every deal WON that month, all estimators. Lost
  deals and won deals with no value are not counted.
  Card: the total for the period. Line and trend line as the others.

Both are exactly the estimator scorecard's "All estimators" cards - Total
value of work priced and Total value of work secured. The two rules now live
in ONE file, lib/precontractTotals.js, and the estimator scorecard was
switched onto it, so the two pages cannot disagree. Its figures are
unchanged: same rules, moved.

A month reached with nothing priced or won is a real 0. A month not reached
yet is blank.


THE SECOND "SECURED" CARD - REMOVED
"Value of secured work - as at month end" (added in 1029) is gone. It was a
separate request at the time - the order book at each month end, a stock
rather than a flow - but it was never connected, and Value of work secured is
the one secured-work card now. Any target typed on it is left in the data,
unread.

in April (122,000), a zero-value win, a lost deal and last year's win (none
counted).
