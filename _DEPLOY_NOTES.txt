WHAT COUNTS
  Water Ingress Reports where "Who is responsible for the water ingress?" is
  answered "Rock". Main Contractor, Other Trade, End User and Inconclusive are
  not counted. Drafts are not counted.

  EVERY REPORT COUNTS. A second report on the same project is a second leak -
  the first should have fixed it. No de-duplication.

  THE DATE ON THE FORM decides the month: "Date on which the water ingress
  survey and assessment took place". A report typed up late still lands in
  the month it happened. If that date was left blank, the date submitted is
  used and the report is marked * in the table.

THE CARD
  Big number   the total for the selected period - FY2026 to date by default.
  Graph        the count each month. A month with none is 0 (a real zero); a
               month not reached yet is blank.
  Colour       against the target, LOWER is better.

CLICK A MONTH - on the graph, or a number in the trend table - and a table
opens under the card: date on form, project, who reported it, who surveyed
it, the cause, and "Open report" to the full report in Operations -> Forms.
Click the month again or Close to shut it.


READ FROM THE FORM, NOT HARDCODED
The responsibility question and the date are found from the form definition,
so editing the form in the forms builder does not break the count. "Our fault"
is that question's FIRST option - "Rock" on Rock's form, and whatever another
company's copy says on theirs. Matching the word "Rock" would have counted
nothing for anyone else.

The shared "form date" rule (lib/formDates.js) was deliberately NOT changed:
it does not recognise this form's date label, and widening it would change
how Forms Missing scores other forms.

Checked by running the route on the real form definition with: two leaks on
one project in March (2), a Main Contractor and an Inconclusive report (not
counted), a draft (not counted), a late-typed report (counted in the month on
the form), and one with no date (submission date, marked).
