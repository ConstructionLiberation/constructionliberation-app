so they cannot drift apart again.

A FIX ON THE ESTIMATOR CARDS
Their version built its window with dates converted to UTC, which in UK summer
time and in New Zealand ended the window a day early - a project won on the
last day of a month was left out. Shown with test data: one estimator's figure
read 300,000 instead of the correct 200,000. The shared rule works in plain
months. Estimator figures may move slightly where that happened; they are now
right.
