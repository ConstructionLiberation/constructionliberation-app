Admin > Email Notifications now has three tabs (admin only, as before):

1. TASK REMINDERS - the reminders set up here. Unchanged.

2. REGISTER - every email the portal sends automatically (37), in two groups,
   Scheduled (12) and When something happens (25). Each row: what it is for,
   when / how often, who receives it, and when it was last sent. Where the
   recipients are set by role or job title, the people it reaches TODAY are
   listed (e.g. WIP complete -> the active Accounts users); if nobody matches,
   it says so in amber - that email goes to no one.

3. SENT LOG - every email actually sent, newest first: when, to (and cc),
   subject, what it was for, and Sent / Failed with the reason. Search by
   recipient or subject; filter sent / failed; last 24 hours to everything
   kept. The most recent 3,000 are kept. It starts recording from this deploy.

HOW THE LOG WORKS
Every portal email goes to one address, the email service. The server's
request function is wrapped once: any call to that address is passed through
UNTOUCHED and its result recorded in the company's own database, with the route
or scheduled job that sent it. So every email is logged - including any added
later - without changing 30-odd senders. Recording can never stop an email:
every step is guarded, and the response is cloned so the sender still reads it.
"Sent" means the email service accepted it, not that it was opened.

WHEN AN EMAIL IS ADDED TO THE PORTAL, ADD IT TO lib/emailRegister.js. The log
records it regardless.
