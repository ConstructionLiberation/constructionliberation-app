PACKAGE 1081 - "Forms due this week": copied to each project's Contracts Manager

Two files. Complete replacements. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1080.

  pages/api/cron/forms-weekly-notify.js
  lib/emailRegister.js                  (the Register's description of it)

"Rock Roofing - Forms due this week (w/c ...)" - the Monday digest to each site
supervisor - is now COPIED (cc) to the Contracts Manager of every project
listed in it. A supervisor covering two projects with different CMs: both CMs
are copied.

Each project line now names its CM, e.g.
  J240 - Market Drayton (CM: Will McVeigh)
so a CM copied on a digest covering several projects can see which are theirs.

Nobody is copied to themselves: where the CM is also the supervisor, they get
the one email.

The Pre-Start digest to CMs ("Up and coming Pre-Start Notifications") is
unchanged. The Register tab's "who receives it" is updated, and the Sent log
shows the cc.

Checked: the job run with three projects - Steve's digest (two projects, CMs
Will and Mike) sent to Steve, cc Will and Mike, each line naming its CM; Will's
digest for the project he supervises AND manages sent to him alone, no cc. The
job and the daily run that calls it bundle.
