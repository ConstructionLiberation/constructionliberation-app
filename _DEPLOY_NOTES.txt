WHY IT DID NOT OPEN ON THE FINANCIAL YEAR
The year start was read from a field on the tenant record. When that field is
missing the page falls back to the last 12 months - which is the November-to-
October view you kept seeing. A setting that only works after hand-editing JSON
in Upstash is the wrong place for it.


NOW
The year start is stored in the company's own database
(config:financial-year-start) and set ON THE SCORECARD:

  1. First visit after deploy: a yellow bar at the top -
     "Set your financial year once. It starts on the 1st of [month] [Save]"
  2. Choose December, Save.
  3. The page reloads on 1 Dec 25 to 30 Nov 26 and opens there every time.

Nothing is guessed: until it is saved the page says so and shows the last 12
months.


THE GRAPHS
With the financial year range, every graph and the trend table have all twelve
columns, December to November. Months still to come are there and empty - not
zero, not missing.

Checked by running the route: not set -> last 12 months; 13 refused; December
saved; reopened on Dec 25 -> Nov 26, 12 columns, Oct and Nov empty.

3. Reload - still Dec to Nov, no yellow bar.
