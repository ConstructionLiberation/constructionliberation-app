PACKAGE 1082 - Site App: Forms Completed set up like the portal's Completed page

One file. Complete replacement. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1081.

  pages/forms/cm/forms-completed.js

Contracts Manager > Forms Completed on the Site App no longer asks for a
project first. It opens straight onto every form submitted on the CM's projects
- or on every live project, where the CM has been given all-projects access
(the same rule as every CM screen).

  DAYS       Today / Yesterday / Last 7 days / Last 30 days / Custom (From-To).
             Opens on Last 7 days. Forms are grouped under a heading per day -
             Today, Yesterday, Tuesday 29 September... - newest first.
  FILTERS    Project (the CM's projects) and Form type, with a count.
  EACH FORM  form type, project, operative, the time submitted, and any flags.
             Tap to open it, as before.

Days are LOCAL: a form submitted at 23:40 is under that day, not the next.
(The portal's Completed page reads typed dates as midnight UTC, which shifts
them an hour in UK summer time - not copied here.)

Site issues are not listed (as on the portal page).

Checked in a simulated browser with forms across two of the CM's projects,
another CM's project, a site issue, a late-evening form and older forms:
opens on Last 7 days grouped Today (2) / Yesterday (1); the 23:40 form stays
under Yesterday; Today, Yesterday and Last 30 days each right (a 12-day-old
form under its weekday heading); the project and form-type filters correct;
the other CM's project and the issue excluded; the project list offers only
the CM's projects. All 158 pages pass the browser check.
