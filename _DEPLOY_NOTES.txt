pkg923 - Missing Forms: the cards now come from the server, not a second sum
=============================================================================

REPLACE (1 file, existing - no new files to create):

  pages/operations/forms-missing.js

Deploy after pkg920.


WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------
pkg920 recalculated the card totals IN THE BROWSER after a tick, from the
rows it already had. That was a second copy of the counting rule - the exact
shape behind most of what we have found this month - and it meant that when
the cards did not move there was no way to tell whether the waive had failed
or the copy had simply disagreed with the server.

The server already counts this correctly. Your screenshot proves it: 33%,
1/3 done, 2 missing IS the post-waive figure. The persistence worked; the
number on screen was the problem.

So the browser no longer counts anything. After a tick it re-runs the same
GET the page loads with, and the cards show exactly what the server counted.
One rule, on the server.

It refreshes WITHOUT the loading flag on purpose: the pop-out is rendered
inside the loading conditional, so calling load() would shut the modal in
your face mid-tick.


TWO THINGS THAT DID NOT AGREE, ALSO FIXED
------------------------------------------
- The header said "28 rows" while the cards said 27 required - the row
  count still included the waived one.
- The main table below the cards still listed waived rows, so a form you had
  just marked not needed reappeared in the list you were working through.
  They are hidden there now, and remain visible and undoable in the
  "Not needed" section of the pop-out.


AFTER DEPLOYING - CHECK
-----------------------
1. Open Pre-Start. Tick one of the two missing rows.
2. The card percentage, the "x/y done" line, the overall card and the row
   count should ALL move immediately, without a refresh.
3. Untick it from Not needed - everything moves back.
4. The waived row should not appear in the table under the cards.
