A "SUPPLIER EMAIL" box on the PO - in the portal (under the supplier) and in
the Site App (under the chosen supplier). It fills in from the supplier's
Xero contact and can be changed for this PO (a branch, a named rep); it is
saved with the PO - kept through drafts, raising, approval and a CM's edits -
and the PO is emailed there (the portal's send box can still adjust it at the
last moment). Picking the supplier again refills it only if nobody has typed
their own. A malformed address is refused.

Checked through the real routes: from Xero when nothing typed; a typed
address kept on raising, through a draft saved and raised, and the email sent
to it; a malformed one refused; an operative's typed address kept, the CM's
edit keeping it and changing it. In a REAL browser (Chromium): the portal box
filled from Xero, a typed address sticking when the supplier is picked again;
the Site App box (phone size) filled from Xero, typed address kept, placed
below the supplier. All server routes compile; all pages pass the browser
check. Built from your deployed packages (1022-1213, verified against their
zips).
