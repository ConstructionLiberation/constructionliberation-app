PACKAGE 1062 - Org charts: dotted lines go around cards

Three files. Complete replacements. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1061.

  lib/orgTree.js                (routeAround - the router)
  components/OrgTree.js         (screen)
  components/orgChartImage.js   (PNG)

Dotted lines no longer pass behind cards. They route AROUND every card on the
chart, keeping a small clear margin, still in straight runs at right angles:

  - Neighbours on a row: one straight line.
  - Cards in the way: the line goes round them - e.g. dropping into the gap
    between rows, across, and back up (right, down, right, up, right).
  - Different levels: through the gaps - e.g. right, down, right.
  - A line can leave and arrive on whichever side of a card gives the
    cleanest route; fewer bends are preferred over a slightly shorter line.

How: every card is an obstacle; the router searches a grid of the gaps
between cards for the shortest route, with a penalty for each bend. If no
route exists (cards packed with no gap), it falls back to the 1061 straight
route rather than drawing nothing.

The screen and the PNG use the same router, so the download matches what you
see. Lines may run along the gaps where the reporting lines are; they stay
dashed so the two are told apart.

Checked on a realistic chart (a director, a row of four, a row of three):
neighbours; same row with two cards between; down a level past cards; across
and down past cards; director to the far bottom; stacked. Every route: only
horizontal and vertical runs, never through a card, reaching both cards, in
3-12 ms. The PNG draws no curves; the org chart pages bundle; all 158 pages
pass the browser check.
