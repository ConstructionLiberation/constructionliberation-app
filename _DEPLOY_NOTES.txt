1. CURRENT ORG CHART - ARRANGED BY HAND
================================================================================
The chart is now a proper tree: who reports to whom, with connector lines.

WHO is on it stays automatic - every active portal login, plus the outsourced
services table. WHERE they sit is now yours to set. Only the layout is saved
(mgmt:org:layout); nothing about a person is copied.

  - First visit: everyone is in the tray "Not placed yet". Nobody is put in
    a position by guesswork - the old job-role tiers are gone.
  - New login: appears in the tray, ready to place.
  - Someone leaves: drops off; anyone under them moves up to report to whoever
    they reported to. The team stays together.
  - Job role changed in Admin: the card updates; its position is kept.

"Arrange chart" switches editing on. Two ways to move someone:

  DRAG (desktop) a card onto another card:
     middle of the card      -> UNDER that person
     left or right edge      -> BESIDE them (an amber bar shows which side)
     the bar at the top      -> top level
     the tray at the bottom  -> off the chart (their team moves up a level)

  CLICK a card (works on phone and tablet too): a panel with
     Reports to [dropdown], Move left, Move right, Take off chart.

Putting someone under a person who reports to them is refused with a message.

Outsourced services now go ON the chart like anyone else - dashed, grey,
tagged OUTSOURCED - and are placed the same way. The table's "Managed By"
column is gone because placing the card says it; any value already saved is
kept in the data.


2. 1-YEAR ORG CHART - NEW PAGE
================================================================================
Its own nav item. A plan, so nobody comes from the logins - every card is
added by hand: "+ Add person or role" with Name (blank if not known yet),
Role, Status and Notes.

  Status   Existing  - solid, red top (as the current chart)
           New role  - dashed, green, tagged NEW ROLE
           Vacancy   - dashed, amber, tagged VACANCY

"Copy current chart" fills it once with today's people in today's positions
as a starting point. It asks before replacing anything already there. After
that the two charts are independent.

Arranged exactly like the current chart. In Arrange mode, clicking a card
also offers "Edit details" (rename, change status, delete). Deleting a card
moves anyone under it up a level.

Stored whole at mgmt:org:future.


BOTH CHARTS
================================================================================
  - Download chart (PNG) draws the tree as shown, titled and dated.
  - Saves carry the version they started from. If someone else rearranged
    the chart in between, the move is refused with their name and the chart
    reloads - one layout never silently replaces another.
