Click a month - a point on the graph, or a number in the trend table - and a
pop-out opens with what makes up that figure. Same style as the pre-contract
scorecard's pop-outs: dark backdrop, title and count, fixed header, Escape or x
to close; a stray click behind it does not close it.

  Value of work priced (+ existing)    each value change: project, organisation,
                                       estimator, previous, new, change, date
                                       - total change at the foot
  Value of work secured (+ existing)   each project won: project, organisation,
                                       value, estimator, sales person, stage,
                                       lead source, decision date - total at foot
  Strike rate on value (+ existing)    every project DECIDED in the rolling six
                                       months, Won or Lost - "Won X of Y decided
                                       = Z%" at the foot
  Negotiating pipeline                 each project in Negotiating at that month
                                       end, with its value then - total at foot
  Payless notices                      each credit note: job, project, credit
                                       note no, invoice, customer, date, amount.
                                       If the month was adjusted by hand on the
                                       Commercial Scorecard, the pop-out says so
                                       (e.g. counted as 1, not 2)
  Water ingress                        now in the same pop-out (it was a table
                                       under the card): date on form, project,
                                       reported by, surveyed by, cause, Open report

EVERY POP-OUT ADDS UP TO ITS POINT ON THE GRAPH. The rows come from the same
lists the figures are summed from, and the total line is worked from the rows
shown. Checked across 80 metric-months of test data: no mismatches.

Months with nothing behind them are not clickable. Underlined-dotted numbers in
the trend table are.

The pre-contract scorecard is unchanged - it already had its pop-outs.

Checked: the pop-out totals against the graph (80 metric-months, 0
mismatches); the payless adjustment note; the pop-out rendered with rows,
footer and note; all 157 pages pass the browser check.
