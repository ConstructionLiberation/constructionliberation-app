ON THE PRICE LIST (company-wide)
  "Increase / discount": tick one or more suppliers, a % (minus for a
  discount), a date, a note -> applied to every product from them.
  A banner at the top: "Rates include +10% increase for Renolit from
  12/10/2026 - Price rise letter"   [Remove]
  Each affected price shows e.g. "+10% on 185.85".
  - The supplier's LIST price is kept underneath; rates are worked out on top
    of it, so Remove is exact. Editing a product edits its list price.
  - Several on one supplier COMPOUND (+10% then +5% = +15.5%).
  - Downloads (CSV / Excel) give the LIST prices - the increases stay in the
    app, so a file sent round again never has them twice.
  - UPLOADING a new list: if a supplier in the file has an increase or discount
    in force, you are asked, per supplier, whether to keep applying it - untick
    it if their new list already includes the rise. Others stay. The upload is
    compared with the list prices, so an increase never shows every product of
    that supplier as "changed".
  Build-ups and estimates price with the adjusted rates automatically.

ON ONE QUOTE - "This quote's price adjustments" on the take-off
  e.g. Renolit -10% for this project: on top of the price list, this estimate
  only (the take-off, the pop-out, the totals, the quote); the price list is
  not changed. A note under the settings shows what is in force. Revisions
  record it - "Changes since Rev A" lists it.
