ESTIMATING > LABOUR RATES - works exactly like the Price List:
  ONE rate per labour item: the price per m2, m or nr the estimate uses.
  Where a rate came from (e.g. the installer who quoted) is in NOTES - for
  reference only, never used in pricing.
  Upload (Excel or CSV) -> checked and summarised -> Import. Download CSV /
  Excel / Blank template. Sections, Needs attention, search, edit, add, delete.

THE DAY RATE - a setting at the top of the page
  Not set   amber "Day rate - not set yet". Type the day rate and hours a day
            (8 shown, not saved until Save) -> the hourly rate appears.
  Set       "Day rate 325.00 - 8 hours a day -> 40.63 an hour", who set it, when.
  Every estimate will START from it and can change it. Never filled in for a
  company - they set it. Uploads and edits of the rates leave it alone.
