TOTAL SALES - AVERAGE PER MONTH
Under the total: "110,000 average per month" - the total over the same
finished months, divided by how many there are.


THE GEAR (⚙)
On all four scorecard pages - six scorecards, each with its own layout:

  Business Scorecard                  beside From / To
  Operations - Contracts Manager      beside the tabs (changes with the tab)
  Operations - Operations Manager     beside the tabs
  Pre-Contract - Estimator            beside the tabs (changes with the tab)
  Pre-Contract - Sales                beside the tabs
  Commercial Scorecard                beside "Commercial Team"

Click it: every metric, in order, with a tick to show/hide and arrows to move
it up or down. Each change saves at once and applies for EVERYONE. "Reset to
default" puts the scorecard back to the code's order with nothing hidden.
Hiding or moving a metric changes the cards and the trend table together.

WHO: management and admin. Everyone else sees no gear - and the server
refuses the change too, not just the screen.

CHANGE TO NOTE - PRE-CONTRACT: its old "Metrics" button is replaced by the
gear. That button let ANYONE on the page hide metrics; now it is management
and admin, the same as hiding a person. Also, Estimator and Sales used to
share ONE hidden list (hiding "Total value of work priced" on one hid it on
the other); each now has its own. Until a layout is first saved, both start
from the old shared list, so nothing appears or disappears on deploy.

COMMERCIAL SCORECARD: its six cards were written out one by one, so they were
moved into a list to make them reorderable. Each card's content is unchanged
- checked by comparing the code with only the list markers removed:
identical.

A metric added to a scorecard later appears after the saved ones rather than
staying hidden. Stored at scorecard:layouts, through /api/targets.


Checked: the layout ordering rules (saved order, a new metric, a stale key,
bad stored data); the targets route (management saves, post-contract refused,
two scorecards saved independently, unknown scorecard refused, reset, read);
the gear rendered for an editor and a non-editor; the average per month
through the route (1,100,000 over 10 months = 110,000). Every changed page
bundles against the full repo.
