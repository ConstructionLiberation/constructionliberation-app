ATTACHMENTS - an "Attachments" section on a PO (drawings, specifications,
schedules), separate from the access route documents. Both are ATTACHED TO THE
EMAIL to the supplier as files (fetched through links that last minutes - the
files never become public) and listed on the supplier's page. Attach once the
PO is saved as a draft or raised, until it is sent.
SITE CONTACT - type to search: part of a name or a number; pick from the
matches (Enter picks the first); Escape keeps the chosen one.
ORDER CONFIRMATIONS - "Change default" (admins) beside the box: changes the
company default for every new PO, and this PO's box if it still showed the
old default.
FIX - PO NUMBERING: saving PO settings put the number counter back to the
stored start, so changing anything there (e.g. the confirmations address)
after POs were raised would have REISSUED numbers already used. Now "Next
number" shows the real next number and the counter only moves when that
number is changed.
