PACKAGE 1154 - Revisions keep their own prices; editing one never wipes the draft

Four files. Complete replacements. No re-seed. No env changes. Built on 1153.

  lib/estimate.js, lib/materials.js
  pages/estimating/estimate/[id].js      (folder literally named [id])
  pages/api/estimating/estimates.js

PRICES FROZEN WITH EACH REVISION. Issuing now stores, with the revision, the
price-list entries its lines used (each product and labour rate, after
supplier increases). Wherever that revision is priced, its own entries win -
a later price-list change never alters it:
  - viewing it (its stored figures, as since 1151)
  - EDITING it - priced as at its issue; a blue banner says so, with "Use
    today's prices" to re-price it deliberately
  - "Changes since Rev X" - compared at Rev X's own prices, so a price-list
    change now SHOWS as a change
The current draft follows the price list as before. Revisions issued before
this deploy did not store their prices, so they can only be priced at today's
(their window says so).

EDITING A REVISION NEVER WIPES THE DRAFT. "Edit Rev A" PARKS the draft instead
of replacing it. "Working on: [Rev A - editing] [Draft Rev B]" at the top
switches between them - each keeps its own changes. Re-issuing Rev A replaces
Rev A (same letter) and brings the draft back. The rules stay: only the latest
revision, only if not sent. Issuing the draft as a new revision while an edit
of the previous one is parked sets that edit aside (it is no longer the
latest) - the message says so.

Checked through the real route, with a real 20% price rise in the middle: Rev
A 1,107.00 with its price (185.85) stored; price raised to 223.02; Edit Rev A
-> qty 100 still priced 1,107.00, the draft (150) parked; switch to the draft
-> 150 at today's price (1,992.00), Rev A parked at its edit (110); back ->
110 kept; re-issue -> Rev A (1,217.70, its own price), the draft back (150,
letter B); issuing C with an edit of B parked -> set aside. In a REAL browser
(Chromium): "Working on: Rev A - editing | Draft Rev B"; the banner; the total
at Rev A's own price (21,594.00, its VCL price higher) -> Use today's prices
-> 20,804.00; clicking the draft sends the switch. All server routes compile;
all 165 pages pass the browser check. Built from your deployed packages
(1022-1153, verified against their zips).
