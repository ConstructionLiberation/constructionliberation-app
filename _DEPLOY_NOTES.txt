PACKAGE 1007 - why your bookkeeper's retention figures differ from yours
================================================================================

Two files. Complete replacements. ROCK-SIDE - this is a live fault, not a
demo one. No re-seed.

  pages/api/retention.js
  pages/retention.js        <- includes 970, 971 and 982


WHAT WAS HAPPENING
================================================================================
The Retention tab in Bookkeeping is an iframe of /retention?embed=1 - the
same page you look at. So the two cannot disagree unless something in it
varies by USER, and it did.

/api/retention was guarded:

    requireRole(req, res, ['post-contract','management','admin'])

`accounts` is not on that list. Your bookkeeper's call was refused with a
403. The page then did:

    const d1 = await r1.json()
    setEntries(d1.entries || [])

No status check. A 403 has no `entries`, so `|| []` turned the refusal into
AN EMPTY LIST OF MANUAL ENTRIES - and the page carried on and rendered the
Xero-derived rows from /api/dashboard, which they CAN read.

So they were not seeing wrong figures. They were seeing the retention tracker
with every hand-added entry missing, and nothing on screen to say so. Any
project that exists only as a manual entry, and any manual correction to a
Xero row, was absent for them and present for you.

The lessons file already has this exact shape written down: "a bookkeeper
said a page did not load - a 403 swallowed by catch {}". Same role, same
swallow, different page.


THE FIX, IN TWO PARTS
================================================================================
1. READING AND WRITING ARE NOW DIFFERENT PERMISSIONS.

       READ   post-contract, accounts, management, admin
       WRITE  post-contract, management, admin

   Accounts read; they do not write. The entries are the post-contract team's
   record and the Bookkeeping tab is in VIEW_ONLY_TABS - view-only was always
   the intent, the guard just did not express it.

   pre-contract and designer still cannot read it. Unchanged.

2. A REFUSAL IS NO LONGER RENDERED AS DATA.

   The page now checks the response and shows a banner - in embed mode too,
   because the embed is exactly where this was seen and where nothing
   explained it. A 403 says permission and what to ask for; anything else
   names the status. Either way it says the figures below are incomplete
   rather than quietly implying they are not.


WHY THIS MATTERS BEYOND ONE PAGE
================================================================================
Second time tonight a swallowed response cost real time - the variations
tracker was the other, where "it doesn't save" took four packages because the
409 never reached the screen. Both are `catch {}` or `|| []` turning a refusal
into a plausible answer.

Worth a sweep of its own: every fetch that does `|| []` on a response it never
checked. Not tonight.


SMOKE TEST
================================================================================
1. Have your bookkeeper open Bookkeeping > Retention. The figures should now
   match yours exactly.
2. Confirm they cannot EDIT anything there - it is view-only, and the API now
   refuses their writes explicitly rather than by omission.
3. Your own Retention tracker and Bookkeeping mirror: unchanged.
4. If you have a pre-contract user, check they still cannot read it and now
   see the banner rather than a silently thinner tracker.
