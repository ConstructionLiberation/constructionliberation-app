THE REPORT PDF (the download and the customer's):
  1. MARK-UP NOW SITS OVER THE PLACES IT WAS DRAWN. The PDF drew the drawing's
     WHOLE SHEET unrotated, while the viewer shows the page's VISIBLE AREA (its crop
     box - architects' drawings are often a window inside a bigger sheet) at its
     ROTATION - so the drawing came out shrunk into a corner with the mark-up over
     the wrong places (PR-0027). It now draws exactly what the viewer shows.
     Text marks also came out far too big - the PDF assumed a narrow viewer; a text
     mark now records the width it was drawn at (marks made before this are taken
     as drawn in the full-screen window).
  2. THE DRAWINGS COME BEFORE THE PHOTOS - straight after the notes, each on its own
     page; the photos carry on after them.
  3. LINE BREAKS KEPT - works completed, site communications and your own sections
     (numbered lines now stay on their own lines).
  4. VARIATIONS AS A TABLE - No. / Description / Instructed / Total; YES in GREEN,
     NO in RED; long descriptions wrap in their column.
