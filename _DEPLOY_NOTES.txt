pkg915 - Retention tracker: "Gross AFA" renamed to "Proj. Final Account"
=========================================================================

REPLACE (1 file, existing - no new files to create):

  pages/retention.js

DEPLOY AFTER pkg914, which renames the matching label on the Applications
page and the PDF. Either order works, but together they are the change.


WHAT AND WHY
------------
It was one figure under two names. "Gross AFA" on the tracker and "Proj.
Final Account" on the Applications page are the same number on the same
basis - measured contract sum plus variations at final value, before MCD.

Renamed everywhere it is visible: the column header, its tooltip, the
"where it comes from" drill-down heading, the three rows inside it and the
sentence that compares it to project details.

The column NEXT to it is still "Final Account" and still means something
different - that one is after MCD, and it is the figure retention is
calculated on. Its tooltip now reads "Proj. Final Account less MCD" so the
relationship between the two columns is stated on screen.


IMPORTS STILL WORK
------------------
The CSV importer now accepts "Proj. Final Account", "Gross AFA" and
"afaGross" for the same field. Spreadsheets already in circulation carry the
old header, and an import that quietly reads null does not fail - it wipes
the figure.


AFTER DEPLOYING - CHECK
-----------------------
1. Retention tracker: first money column reads "Proj. Final Account", same
   figures as before. J178 should still show 351,649.77.
2. Open the drill-down on a project and confirm nothing still says Gross AFA.
3. Compare J178 against its application: Proj. Final Account should be the
   same number on both pages. That is the point of the change.


NOT CHANGED
-----------
Nothing calculates differently. Internal field names (afaGross) are
untouched - renaming a stored key is a migration, and this is a label.
