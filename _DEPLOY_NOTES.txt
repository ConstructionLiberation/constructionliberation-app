The weather on a Project Report now always covers the 14 DAYS UP TO AND
INCLUDING THE REPORT DATE, whenever the previous report was (1067 used the
days since the previous report).

Finished days only: a report dated today shows the 13 days to yesterday and
fills in the 14th when reopened. Labelled "the 14 days to this report's date"
on the report, and "Weather - 14 days to report date" in the PDF.

Draft reports pick this up when reopened or with Refresh weather. COMPLETED
reports keep the weather they were issued with, by design.

the last report date), dated today (13 finished days), an older report (14
days); a PDF regenerated with the new heading; the report page and routes
